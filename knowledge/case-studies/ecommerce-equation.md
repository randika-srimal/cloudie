### Challenge

Ecommerce Equation faced challenges with fragmented systems and manual processes. Their existing CRM setup couldn’t support their sales growth ambitions, causing lead management and reporting inefficiencies. The lack of seamless integration with external tools like Stripe and Aircall limited their ability to streamline sales processes and accurately track leads.

Additionally, their manual data handling created inconsistencies in reporting and customer segmentation, affecting lead qualification and marketing efforts. They needed a comprehensive solution to unify sales, marketing, and reporting while preserving data integrity during migration.

### Solution

We implemented a complete HubSpot onboarding solution, integrating multiple systems into a centralised HubSpot CRM. This involved migrating 16,000+ contact records from CMM to HubSpot and establishing custom properties for seamless lead tracking and reporting. The data migration was conducted carefully to avoid duplicates and ensure data accuracy.

We introduced automation workflows to manage lead qualification and nurture journeys, aligning sales and marketing efforts for optimal customer experience. Marketing automation workflows included:

* Email nurturing based on customer type
* Event-based communication, including registration confirmation, check-ins and post-event communications.
* Sales-driven automation that reduced the admin and friction involved with handling leads
* Real-time lead routing and scoring

Custom integrations with Zapier and Humanitix enabled Ecommerce Equation to track event-based leads in real time.

The three sales pipelines were customised for Web Apps, Strategy Sessions, and the US market. Automated deal stages and custom dashboards were built to provide visibility into lead performance, sales velocity, and MQL conversion rates.

We also facilitated integration with external tools such as Stripe for payment processing and Aircall for call tracking, ensuring smooth sales operations. Training sessions for marketing and sales teams empowered them to leverage HubSpot’s full potential.

### Results

The project successfully centralised E-commerce Equation’s sales and marketing operations into HubSpot, transforming their business processes. The implementation of automation workflows reduced manual workload and increased lead response time.

Data migration and integration with external tools enabled accurate reporting and better decision-making. The new nurture journeys enhanced customer engagement, resulting in an increase in email open rates and an improvement in conversion rates.

The sales team benefited from the customised pipelines and automated follow-up sequences, closing deals faster and tracking opportunities more effectively. The integration with Stripe and Aircall streamlined payment processing and customer communication, leading to a more cohesive customer experience.

### Impact

In short, the impact of the project was immediate. Sales and marketing teams experienced a noticeable reduction in repetitive tasks, allowing them to focus on strategic activities like customer engagement and lead conversion. The data integrity improvements facilitated trust in reporting, empowering better strategic decisions.

In the long term, Ecommerce Equation’s unified CRM setup positioned them for scalable growth. The automation workflows for lead nurturing and segmentation created a consistent customer journey, improving customer retention rates. Integrations with Stripe and Aircall provided seamless payment and communication processes, enhancing the end-user experience.

The ability to track leads in real time and manage multiple pipelines simultaneously has significantly increased the efficiency of the sales team. With comprehensive training and ongoing support, Ecommerce Equation can now leverage HubSpot’s full potential for future growth.
