### Summary of the business:

Swagelok ANZ is a trusted provider of fluid system products and solutions, servicing Australia and New Zealand’s industrial and energy sectors. They needed a modern CRM platform that could support their complex sales structure, account planning, and visibility across territories and project types.

### The Problem

#### Problem 1: Swagelok had relied on SAP as both CRM and ERP, but the sales team found it clunky, admin-heavy, and not suited for proactive engagement or strategic planning.

Problem 2: Lack of integration, stakeholder mapping, and clean reporting meant the team couldn't collaborate effectively or forecast pipeline activity across MRO and construction projects.

### The Solution

A scalable CRM to empower team selling

Engaging.io conducted stakeholder interviews, ran discovery sessions across Australia and New Zealand, and gathered wishlists from users, managers, and executives alike. These insights were used to architect a flexible CRM in HubSpot Sales Hub Enterprise that aligned to Swagelok's dual sales motion: ongoing MRO account engagement and long-cycle construction project bidding.

We configured custom properties, dynamic deal stages, conditional logic, and advanced workflows to ensure data integrity and smart automations. Through our collaboration with Vision33, we mapped SAP data into HubSpot and prepared for a one-way sync that would evolve to bidirectional sync later. We also deployed a sandbox environment for testing, delivered targeted training, and structured team permissions for secure and streamlined user access.

The Strategic Plan:

* **Sales Process Redesign:** Mapped multiple sales motions including construction project sales and MRO servicing. This involved defining pipeline stages unique to each stream, consolidating best practices from multiple teams, and unifying them into a scalable CRM framework.
* **Workflow Automation:** Designed and deployed workflows for lead assignment based on territory and account priority. Trigger-based automations were also implemented to surface stalled deals, auto-update properties at key stage changes, and send internal alerts for large opportunities.
* **SAP Integration Planning:** Worked in collaboration with Vision33 to scope the mapping of key SAP fields (e.g. customer credit limits, order status, account classification) to HubSpot. Defined the one-way sync logic and identified future opportunities for bidirectional data enrichment.
* **Data Migration:** Conducted manual cleansing of outdated and duplicate contact records, standardised formatting across AU/NZ, and applied governance around company naming conventions. All data was securely imported using HubSpot’s import tools and reviewed post-import.
* **Training + Admin Enablement:** Delivered a phased training program starting with Admin Super Users. Created sandbox replicas for test learning, deployed permission tiers, and built training materials to support onboarding of future users. Established documentation for workflow governance and permission audits.

The Implementation:

* **Discovery Sessions:** Conducted AU/NZ-wide stakeholder workshops to gather cross-functional insights across sales, operations, and leadership. Each session aimed to map real-world processes, identify bottlenecks, and build internal buy-in early in the project. These workshops were essential to capturing regional nuance and embedding user voices into solution design.
* **Custom Configuration:** Designed and deployed five bespoke pipelines to support Swagelok's construction project workflows, MRO accounts, and internal team structures. Custom objects, conditional logic, and dynamic field behaviours were created to guide users through process steps, enforce data integrity, and support scalable forecasting. Role-based permission tiers were implemented to balance autonomy with oversight.
* **Integration Readiness:** Worked in close partnership with Vision33 to document SAP field structures, prioritise critical data sets (e.g. account hierarchies, order statuses), and define the optimal Saltbox API sync framework. Created a living integration specification and mapped out phased sync strategies to mitigate risk and support future bidirectional data exchange.
* **Testing + Training:** Delivered sandbox environments to simulate real-world HubSpot usage, enabling user testing and admin experimentation without risk. Rolled out a change management program centred on enablement: admin training, task-based how-tos, and structured Q&A sessions. These were reinforced with documented best practices, guided walkthroughs, and a culture of continuous feedback. This approach ensured the transition was smooth, with minimal resistance and high adoption.

### Impact and Results

Swagelok now operates with a purpose-built CRM that reflects how their business truly functions. Sales teams can track construction bids and MRO activities separately, while managers gain real-time insights into account health, opportunity velocity, and sales performance across regions. Admins have granular control over user access, governance, and data hygiene, allowing them to enforce business rules and streamline process compliance.

The transition has already resulted in measurable time savings and reduced admin overhead. Sales reps now spend less time double-handling data or chasing spreadsheets, and more time engaging clients. Weekly WIPs revealed that stakeholder visibility and cross-team collaboration have significantly improved, particularly with the structured workflows and deal tagging features. Automated lead routing and pipeline triggers have replaced many manual steps, creating a leaner and more accountable sales engine.

This foundational CRM investment has also future-proofed Swagelok’s broader tech roadmap. It de-risks upcoming SAP upgrades by decoupling CRM functionality, creates a scalable blueprint for rolling out Marketing Hub or Service Hub modules, and offers real-time business intelligence that was previously locked in legacy systems. With structured reporting, better visibility, and tailored automations, Swagelok ANZ has not just modernised—they’ve strategically positioned themselves for long-term growth.
