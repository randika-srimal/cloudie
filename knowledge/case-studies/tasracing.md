### Summary of the business:

Tasracing is the principal racing authority in Tasmania and a state-owned company responsible for overseeing and developing the thoroughbred, harness, and greyhound racing industries. Established through state legislation, it manages the strategic direction, funding, promotion, and operations of racing across the state.

The organisation supports race clubs, administers prize money, manages racing venues, and promotes Tasmanian racing to local, national, and international audiences, while also playing a key role in industry integrity and animal welfare standards.

### The Problem

**Fragmented systems and instability were limiting Tasracing’s ability to operate and scale effectively.**
Tasracing was operating within a highly fragmented and increasingly fragile digital ecosystem. Years of layered systems, evolving requirements, and reliance on legacy infrastructure had created a platform that was difficult to manage, prone to failure, and unable to support the organisation’s operational needs at scale.

At the same time, internal restructuring and budget constraints reduced available resources, placing additional pressure on systems to perform reliably without ongoing manual intervention. What was required was not just a rebuild, but a stabilised and future-ready foundation.

**Legacy systems were unstable and impacting critical user experiences.**
Tasracing’s existing WordPress-based websites were experiencing degraded performance, slow load times, and failures across key pages and forms. This created unreliable user journeys and increased operational risk across both public-facing and internal processes.

**Core registration systems lacked reliability and data integrity.**
The animal registration system experienced critical failures in search functionality, alongside missing and inconsistent data fields. This exposed deeper structural issues within the system, limiting its ability to support accurate operations and scalable growth.

### The Solution

To address these challenges, Engaging.io delivered a phased transformation focused on stabilising core systems, consolidating platforms, and introducing scalable infrastructure.

The approach prioritised replacing fragile legacy environments with a unified HubSpot-based ecosystem, while ensuring flexibility for ongoing improvements through a retained support model.

The plan

**HubSpot onboarding and centralised CRM foundation**
A structured HubSpot environment was implemented to centralise data, improve visibility, and support future automation. This created a single source of truth for operations, reducing fragmentation across systems.

**Custom website rebuild on HubSpot CMS**
The existing WordPress websites were replaced with a fully custom HubSpot CMS build, improving performance, reliability, and ease of content management. This enabled Tasracing to manage updates internally without ongoing technical dependency.

**Scalable event management system using HubDB**
A hybrid system was developed using HubDB to manage event data, paired with dynamic page templates for flexible content delivery. This streamlined event creation and ensured consistency across all event experiences.

**Member portal and structured content experience**
A portal-style experience was introduced to support animal owners accessing restricted information and related content. This improved navigation, accessibility, and overall user experience across key journeys.

**Ongoing support and optimisation retainer**
Following implementation, a support retainer was established to address performance issues, resolve technical challenges, and continuously optimise the platform. This ensured long-term stability and adaptability as business needs evolved.

The Implementation

The project was delivered in a phased approach, beginning with discovery sessions to understand Tasracing’s existing systems, infrastructure constraints, and operational workflows. This allowed Engaging.io to identify key failure points across the website, registration systems, and hosting environment, and work with Tasracing and their consultant on a designed solution that balanced immediate stabilisation with long-term scalability.

Initial delivery focused on HubSpot onboarding and foundational setup, including CRM configuration, data structuring, and the rebuild of core website components on HubSpot CMS. In parallel, design and development work was undertaken to create modular templates and a scalable page structure, ensuring the Tasracing team could manage content without ongoing development support.

A key part of the implementation was the development of the Owners portal using HubSpot Memberships. This required the design and build of member specific functionality with overlapping content between the public and restricted areas of the website. Owner membership and restricted content is all managed by Tasracing directly in their HubSpot account.

Following the initial rollout, a second phase expanded website functionality and refined content structures based on evolving requirements. The project then transitioned into an ongoing support retainer, where Engaging.io continued to optimise performance, resolve technical issues, and provide strategic guidance as Tasracing navigated organisational and operational changes.

### Impact and results

**A stabilised digital foundation built for performance, flexibility, and long-term scalability.**

The project delivered a significant shift from unstable, fragmented systems to a more structured and reliable digital ecosystem. By consolidating platforms into HubSpot and replacing legacy websites, Tasracing gained improved performance, greater control over content, and a more consistent user experience across key journeys.

Operationally, the introduction of structured data through HubDB and a centralised CRM reduced manual processes and improved the efficiency of event and content management. This was particularly valuable given internal resource constraints, enabling the team to maintain and update the platform without reliance on multiple systems or external workarounds.

From a technical and strategic perspective, the phased delivery and ongoing support model ensured that Tasracing could continue to evolve its platform over time. Rather than a one-off rebuild, the project established a scalable foundation that supports future enhancements, improved system reliability, and the ability to adapt as organisational needs change.
