### Challenge

This entertainment brand had a bold vision: unify five distinct sub-brands under one flexible and scalable marketing ecosystem. Their legacy email tool limited segmentation, personalisation, and scalability. With fragmented data across ticketing platforms (like ProVenue), analytics (BigQuery), and a separate CRM, the marketing team struggled with data visibility and campaign agility.

They needed a solution that allowed brand-specific marketing while maintaining central oversight, complete with automated workflows, detailed segmentation by geography and behaviour, and real-time reporting. All of this had to be managed without requiring developers for day-to-day marketing execution.

### Solution

We delivered a bespoke onboarding of HubSpot Marketing Hub Enterprise and Operations Hub Professional, structured to support complex, multi-brand operations. Key highlights included:

* **Multi-Brand Setup**: Four Business Units were configured for five brands, each with unique templates, unsubscribe flows, and reporting dashboards.
* **Modular Templates**: Custom, brand-specific templates for email and landing pages ensured consistency with room for creativity.
* **BigQuery Integration:** A one-way custom sync transformed ticketing data into HubSpot deals, enriching contacts with line item info for segmentation and reporting.
* **Workflow Automation**: Created lead nurture, abandonment journeys, and form logic workflows per Business Unit and geography.
* **Training & Enablement**: Delivered tailored, role-based training with cheat sheets and recorded sessions, empowering internal users to be self-sufficient.

### Results

Our clients first campaign using their new HubSpot environment achieved a 16.2% open rate and 3.79% CTR—an impressive start for a newly warmed domain. The BigQuery integration streamlined data visibility, while modular templates and workflow automations enabled the team to launch segmented campaigns without relying on developers or IT. Key outcomes include:

* **IP warming & deliverability success:** 16.2% open rate, 3.79% CTR on first campaign despite a cold domain—showcasing list quality and email relevance.

* **Improved data accessibility:** The one-way BigQuery integration simplified reporting and eliminated reliance on spreadsheets or third-party tools like DataBox.

* **Operational efficiency:** Marketing teams can now independently execute campaigns, and form submissions are automatically routed based on brand and location, reducing delays and improving responsiveness.

* **Centralised and brand-specific insights:** Campaign and ad performance is now measured per BU, not globally, allowing for more precise budget allocation.

* **Cross-sell potential unlocked:** Lifecycle stage configuration and lead scoring now support segmentation based on brand, behaviour, and geography—key for ticket upsells.

### Impact

This implementation gave the client a centralised yet agile MarTech stack. Without increasing headcount or IT dependency, marketing teams can now launch, test, and personalise campaigns at speed. The data integration reduced the burden on analysts, empowering marketers with relevant insights in-platform.

Campaign templates and workflows are now consistent but flexible, boosting both brand integrity and creative freedom. Internal adoption has been high, with teams engaging HubSpot Academy and confidently leveraging new features. The groundwork laid by this transformation supports long-term scalability, enhanced audience engagement, and smarter cross-sell strategies.
