### Challenge

As the enterprise HR and payroll software provider continued to grow, the team set out to enhance their customer support experience with a more streamlined, scalable, and secure approach. With increasing volumes of support interactions, they recognised the need for a centralised system that could automate key processes, prioritise tickets efficiently, and provide faster, more consistent responses.

Their goal was to implement a structured support framework—one that not only improved internal workflows but also elevated the customer experience. A key priority was introducing automation to reduce the reliance on manual ticket routing and categorisation, allowing the support team to focus on resolving queries rather than managing queues.

In parallel, the enterprise HR and payroll software provider aimed to build in a formalised feedback loop to capture service insights and inform continuous improvement. With data security top of mind, especially regarding Personally Identifiable Information (PII), they also needed a platform that ensured compliance while providing full visibility into ticket history, resolution times, and customer satisfaction trends.

### Solution

Our team worked closely with the enterprise HR and payroll software provider to implement an advanced support framework within HubSpot. Key solutions included:

* **Customer Support Centre:** Configured a centralised support hub to manage all customer queries efficiently. Queries were categorised based on customer type (outsourced payroll vs. software customers) and type of issue (functional, technical, training, or account-related).
* **Automated Ticket Prioritisation:** Designed workflows to enable dynamic ticket routing based on query type, eliminating the need for manual prioritisation.
* **Enhanced Customer Portal:** Updated the customer portal to include structured ticket forms, ensuring that necessary details were captured upfront to expedite resolution.
* **Survey Implementation:** Integrated automated weekly customer feedback surveys, allowing the enterprise HR and payroll software provider to monitor service satisfaction and make necessary improvements.
* **Data Security Compliance:** Implemented a structured PII compliance statement and revised ticket submission guidelines to align with Australian privacy legislation.

### Results

* **Reduction in Ticket Resolution Time:** By streamlining query routing and automating priority assignment, customer support teams could address critical issues more efficiently.
* **Higher Customer Satisfaction:** Early feedback from customer surveys indicated an improvement in customer satisfaction scores.
* **Increased Operational Efficiency:** Automation reduced manual ticket handling, allowing support teams to focus on high-value problem-solving.
* **Enhanced Compliance and Security:** Implementation of new data handling protocols reduced the risk of PII exposure.

### Impact

The implementation of a new support system marked a pivotal step forward for the enterprise HR and payroll software provider, delivering a more responsive, efficient, and secure customer service experience. With automated workflows and clearer ticket categorisation now in place, the support team can respond to customer queries more quickly and accurately, reducing wait times and elevating overall satisfaction.

The addition of a structured customer feedback system has brought a new layer of visibility into service performance. Real-time survey insights now inform continuous improvement initiatives, helping the team refine processes and deliver even higher standards of support. Just as importantly, enhanced data handling practices and built-in compliance measures ensure that PII is managed with care, strengthening trust and reinforcing the enterprise HR and payroll software provider’s commitment to data security.

Looking ahead, the upgraded customer portal and streamlined Help Desk setup offer a scalable framework for future growth. The support team now operates with greater clarity, confidence, and control—empowered by real-time insights, optimised workflows, and tools designed for high-impact service delivery.

This transformation has positioned the enterprise HR and payroll software provider to evolve its support offering in line with business growth, without compromising on efficiency, customer experience, or compliance.
