**Summary of the Business**

the luxury hospitality group operates across multiple hospitality venues including a luxury hotel, award-winning restaurant and regenerative farm. ────────────────────────────────────────

**The Problem**
Luxury Experience. Fragmented Technology.

The luxury hospitality group is synonymous with premium hospitality. The guest journey is intentional, curated and high touch. However, behind the scenes, the technology ecosystem did not reflect the same level of sophistication.

Core operational systems were performing their individual functions well, but they were not designed to operate as a unified growth engine. Reservation, dining and guest data lived in separate platforms, limiting visibility across the full customer lifecycle. As a result, marketing, revenue and guest experience teams lacked a single, connected view of performance and opportunity.

**Problem 1**: Reservation Data Locked in Silos
SevenRooms and Opera stored critical booking data independently. Marketing could not easily segment guests based on dining or stay behaviour, nor automate communications based on booking status.

**Problem 2**: Limited Revenue Attribution
Campaign reporting required manual reconciliation. There was no clean way to tie marketing activity directly to confirmed reservations and revenue.

────────────────────────────────────────

**The Solution**

To eliminate fragmentation, Engaging.io designed and deployed a custom integration framework that transformed operational reservation data into structured, revenue-linked objects inside HubSpot.

Rather than relying on static imports or one-way batch syncing, the architecture was built around event-driven automation. Every meaningful reservation change in SevenRooms or Opera became a trigger that updated HubSpot in near real time. This ensured sales, marketing and leadership teams were working from live commercial data rather than delayed reports.

The objective was not simply to “sync data.” It was to convert operational bookings into structured, reportable revenue objects that could power automation, segmentation and attribution.

Our plan was to reposition HubSpot as the luxury hospitality group’s commercial intelligence layer, not just a marketing platform. To achieve this, we focused on transforming operational reservation data into structured, revenue-linked objects inside HubSpot that could power segmentation, automation and attribution.

The priority was alignment. Restaurant and accommodation bookings needed to follow a consistent data model, ensuring that every confirmed reservation, cancellation or modification was reflected accurately in HubSpot. By doing this, we would create a unified pipeline view of real revenue events across dining and stays.

We also focused on data integrity and lifecycle accuracy. That meant ensuring bookings could not create duplicate records, that status changes were reliably reflected in pipeline stages, and that every reservation was correctly associated with the relevant guest profile. Without this foundation, reporting and automation would lack credibility.

Finally, we designed the integration framework to be scalable. The luxury hospitality group required a solution that could support future growth, additional services and evolving guest journeys without requiring structural rework.

With this strategic foundation in place, the next phase was execution: building the integration architecture that would operationalise this model in real time.

**The Strategic Plan**

To resolve the fragmentation between systems, we repositioned HubSpot as the central revenue and intelligence engine for the luxury hospitality group. The solution was not simply about integrating platforms. It was about designing a connected data architecture that translated operational bookings into structured, reportable and automation-ready revenue objects.

We focused on three core outcomes: creating a unified customer view across restaurant and accommodation experiences, enabling behaviour-driven marketing aligned to real booking activity, and establishing closed-loop attribution between marketing investment and confirmed revenue.

By aligning operational systems with a commercial data model inside HubSpot, the luxury hospitality group moved from disconnected reporting and manual reconciliation to a real-time, revenue-led growth platform.

**Centralise all booking data into a unified revenue model**

The first priority was to transform fragmented reservation data into a single, structured revenue model inside HubSpot. This required converting restaurant and accommodation bookings into standardised deal objects, ensuring every confirmed reservation represented a measurable commercial event.

We standardised lifecycle stages across dining and accommodation to reflect true booking status, cancellations and completions. Contact records were consolidated to ensure each guest had a single, unified profile capturing both stay and dining history. This created a clean foundation for reporting, automation and long-term guest value tracking.

**Establish a single customer view across experiences**

Beyond simply syncing data, the objective was to create a holistic guest profile that reflected total engagement with the brand. Dining visits, accommodation stays, spend levels and booking frequency needed to be visible in one place.

This allowed the luxury hospitality group to understand high-value guests across properties and experiences, rather than treating restaurant and hotel interactions as separate relationships. The result was a true single customer view capable of supporting both marketing strategy and revenue optimisation.

**Enable behaviour-based marketing automation**

With structured booking data in place, we focused on enabling automation driven by real guest behaviour rather than static list segmentation.

Booking status, property selection, attendance history and spend thresholds were incorporated into segmentation logic. This enabled nurture flows to trigger from confirmed reservations, pre-arrival milestones, post-visit follow-ups and re-engagement windows. The goal was to align communication timing with the actual guest journey, improving relevance and lifetime value.

**Build closed-loop attribution and marketing ROI visibility**

A core strategic objective was to connect marketing investment directly to confirmed reservations and revenue. To achieve this, we integrated paid media platforms including Meta and Google Ads into HubSpot.

This allowed campaign performance to be measured against real booking data rather than surface-level engagement metrics. Custom dashboards were designed to provide visibility into booking-attributed revenue, cost per reservation and channel-level ROI, giving leadership clear commercial insight into marketing effectiveness.

**Future-proof the data and reporting architecture**

Finally, we ensured the model was scalable. The data structure and reporting framework were designed to support additional properties, new services and evolving guest experiences without reengineering the system.

By focusing on scalability, governance and reporting clarity from the outset, the luxury hospitality group gained a long-term commercial infrastructure rather than a short-term integration fix.

────────────────────────────────────────

**The Implementation**

**Data architecture and integration execution**

To operationalise the strategy, we built a custom middleware layer to manage the flow of reservation data from SevenRooms into HubSpot. Real-time webhook processing ensured that every reservation event, including creations, updates and cancellations, triggered structured updates inside HubSpot without delay.

Deal stage mapping logic was configured to align reservation statuses with pipeline stages, preserving lifecycle accuracy. Historical reservation data was imported via structured CSV migration to ensure reporting continuity from day one. We also implemented custom properties to capture guest tags, preferences and booking attributes, enabling deeper segmentation and reporting precision.

Opera accommodation data was aligned to the same framework, ensuring consistency across dining and hotel revenue objects.

**Marketing infrastructure configuration**

With the data foundation in place, we rebuilt the marketing infrastructure to align with property-level experiences. Subscription types were restructured by property to support compliant, relevant communication preferences.

A lead scoring model was introduced, combining engagement signals with real booking behaviour to prioritise high-value guests. Six automated nurture workflows were deployed, covering pre-arrival communication, post-stay follow-ups, re-engagement sequences and anniversary triggers. Each workflow was designed to reflect real guest lifecycle milestones rather than generic campaign timing.

**Reporting and attribution configuration**

To deliver commercial visibility, we built tailored dashboards for both marketing and revenue stakeholders. The marketing dashboard combined engagement metrics with confirmed booking conversions, while the sales dashboard provided visibility into reservation pipeline stages and realised revenue.

Paid media accounts, including Meta and Google Ads, were connected to enable attribution reporting. This allowed the luxury hospitality group to track booking-attributed revenue by channel and campaign, establishing clear ROI visibility across marketing investment.

**Training and enablement**

To ensure long-term adoption, we delivered structured enablement across teams. Marketing Hub training sessions focused on segmentation, automation management and reporting interpretation. Sales Hub workshops supported reservation pipeline reporting and revenue analysis.

We also rebuilt Service Hub surveys to align with the new data model and provided template design training to ensure consistent, brand-aligned communication execution. This ensured the luxury hospitality group could confidently manage, optimise and scale the platform internally.
