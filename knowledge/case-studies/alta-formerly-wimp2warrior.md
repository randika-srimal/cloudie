Rather than clean up an unmanageable legacy portal, a rapidly growing mixed martial arts programme was onboarded onto an entirely new HubSpot portal through a five-step process covering process mapping, selective data migration, hub configuration with a custom CRM extension, train-the-trainer enablement and testing. Integrations with commercetools, Aircall and Sakari automated order and programme data flow and enabled SMS and outbound calling.

**Tech stack:** commercetools, Sakari, Aircall
