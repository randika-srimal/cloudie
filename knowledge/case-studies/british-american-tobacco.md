### Challenge (150 words)

BAT’s marketing and e-commerce operations relied heavily on Klaviyo and Shopify, but inefficiencies in data synchronization, workflow automation, and reporting created roadblocks. The lack of a unified CRM resulted in fragmented consumer data, making it difficult to track customer interactions and optimize retention strategies.

A key challenge was ensuring seamless data migration without disrupting ongoing marketing campaigns. The project also had to account for age verification compliance, subscription management, and the integration of multiple third-party tools like Yotpo, and Zyber. Additionally, BAT’s strict regulatory environment required a meticulous approach to handling customer data and marketing workflows.

Finally, the project timeline was tight, with BAT needing the project completed within 8 to 10 weeks. This required precise coordination across multiple teams and a phased rollout strategy to ensure minimal operational downtime.

### Solution (250 words)

Engaging.io implemented a comprehensive HubSpot solution, integrating BAT’s customer and order data using Marketing Hub Enterprise, Operations Hub, and Transactional emails. The approach included:

1. **Data Integration & Migration:**
   * Leveraged HubSpot’s native Shopify connector to pull in the order history.
   * Migrated Klaviyo data; manual migration for unsupported data points.
   * Standardized data structures to improve workflow automation.
2. **Workflow Reconstruction:**
   * Rebuilt and optimised 25 lead nurture workflows within HubSpot.
   * Implemented two-way data sync between Shopify and HubSpot to ensure real-time updates.
   * Developed automated processes for customer segmentation and engagement.
3. **Custom Feature Implementation:**
   * Streamlined subscription management by mapping Shopify tags to HubSpot properties.
   * Created a new coupon management process that aligns with BAT’s marketing campaigns through Coupon Carrier.
4. **User Training & Support:**
   * Conducted training sessions for BAT’s marketing and e-commerce teams.
   * Provided a dedicated hypercare phase to ensure smooth adoption.
5. **Improved campaign revenue attribution**
   * Improved reporting around campaigns and the assets contributing to revenue through campaign management and reporting.

This structured approach enabled BAT to transition seamlessly to HubSpot while enhancing automation, compliance, and data-driven marketing.

1. **Enhanced Data Management:**

2. **Workflow Efficiency Gains:**

3. **Marketing Performance Boost:**
