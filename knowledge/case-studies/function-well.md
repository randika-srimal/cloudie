## Blog Case Study Content

### Summary of the business:

Function Well is a holistic fitness and wellness destination based in Brisbane, Queensland, offering multi-zone training, recovery services, and a strong community ethos. With multiple membership tiers and services spanning classes, recovery rooms, and events, the business needed a tech stack that could scale with them—while maintaining a high-touch, premium member experience.

### Challenge

Function Well’s digital ecosystem had outgrown its tech stack. Sales were managed through GymSales, email marketing through Mailchimp, and member data lived in Clubware and MyWellness. These disconnected tools made it difficult to maintain visibility across the member lifecycle, segment by membership type, or run automated nurture and retention campaigns.

Lead follow-up relied on manual task tracking, and sales reporting lacked clarity. The marketing team was constrained by template limitations in Mailchimp and had no single source of truth for campaign engagement or lifecycle progress. With a second location on the horizon and a goal to improve retention and brand loyalty, Function Well needed a unified CRM platform that could handle sales activity, automate comms, and allow segmentation and reporting across thousands of member records — all without compromising their premium service standards.

### Solution

Engaging.io led the full implementation of HubSpot’s Sales and Marketing Hubs, consolidating Function Well’s fragmented systems into a single, intelligent CRM.

We migrated critical functionality from GymSales into a custom-built Lead Pipeline within HubSpot, complete with automated task assignments, lifecycle stages, and sales sequences. Tour booking confirmations, post-enquiry follow-ups, and re-engagement campaigns were rebuilt using HubSpot’s automation tools, ensuring no lead fell through the cracks.

Marketing operations were also overhauled — with Mailchimp fully retired, we created modular email templates, defined marketing subscription types, and built workflows for onboarding, upsell, churn risk, and class/birthday milestones.

We designed and implemented a middleware solution to integrate Clubware and MyWellness. Clubware data now syncs hourly, updating contact records with membership status and visit counts. MyWellness syncs daily to log class and service attendance as timeline events, enabling behaviour-based targeting and retention workflows.

All legacy forms were rebuilt and embedded using HubSpot, with notification routing, lifecycle triggers, and segmentation logic applied. Key reports and dashboards were created for sales tracking, lead source attribution, form performance, and member segmentation.

Training sessions across sales, marketing, and admin teams were delivered using our “Train the Trainer” model, supported by recordings and documentation.

This transformation set Function Well up with a scalable system that reflects their real-world member experience digitally — and future-proofs their processes for growth.

### Results

* **CRM Consolidation:** GymSales and Mailchimp were fully retired, with core functionality replicated or improved in HubSpot — reducing tech costs and simplifying user adoption

* **Sales Process Visibility:** A new Lead Pipeline gives wellness consultants clear visibility over each lead’s stage, source, and follow-up schedule. Tasks and workflows automate key activities, including reminders, re-engagement, and upsell triggers

* **Improved Lead Nurture:** Email workflows were designed for onboarding, tour follow-up, ex-member reactivation, and Base-to-Lifestyle upgrades — several with placeholders and testing in progress for Phase Two

* **Segmentation Ready:** HubSpot now houses over 19,000 records, each segmented by membership tier, lifecycle stage, and activity (e.g. class count, last visit) thanks to our Clubware/MyWellness integration

* **Automated Lifecycle Reporting:** Reports and dashboards surface class attendance trends, email engagement, and sales conversion metrics across lifecycle stages and campaign sources

* **Form Overhaul:** Forms like Book a Tour, Upgrade/Downgrade, Contact Us, and Cancellation now trigger relevant notifications and workflows, removing admin bottlenecks

* **Internal Efficiencies:** Staff now have clarity on task ownership, overdue follow-ups, and lead conversion rates. Marketing can build targeted ads and newsletters using smart lists, while Admin can track critical form submissions in real-time

Early indicators point to improved conversion workflows and deeper personalisation across the member journey, with Function Well poised to activate even more workflows in the next 60–90 days.

### Impact

The impact of this transformation is both operational and strategic.

Function Well’s internal teams — from sales to marketing to admin — now work from a single source of truth. The consolidation of GymSales and Mailchimp into HubSpot removed platform silos, reduced duplicate work, and gave Function Well the infrastructure to scale without adding complexity.

Sales consultants have access to a lead pipeline that reflects their true process, complete with automated reminders, sequence-based follow-ups, and task management. This has already reduced lead leakage and increased accountability. Plus, the shift away from GymSales to HubSpot opened new visibility into lifecycle trends and marketing attribution.

Marketing now operates with clarity and control. Email templates match the refreshed brand, and campaigns are tied to real engagement metrics. The segmentation engine allows Function Well to deliver communications by membership tier, lifecycle stage, and behaviour — not just generic sends to the whole database. The shift from Mailchimp to HubSpot unlocked more automation, better deliverability, and centralised reporting.

Clubware and MyWellness integrations have also changed the game. Instead of a static list of members, Function Well can now see member activity in real time: how often they visit, which classes they attend, and whether they’re due for a re-engagement. This has allowed the team to prioritise leads and members based on real-world data — not guesswork.

With workflows in place (or prepped) for churn risk, milestone rewards, and upgrade nudges, Function Well is now able to support their premium member experience with intelligent, automated CRM logic. The system doesn’t just support their processes — it amplifies them.

### Future Growth

Phase Two includes the activation of workflows that are mapped but pending content — including ex-member nurturing, lifestyle program onboarding, and re-engagement for unconverted leads.

There’s also room to expand SMS automation, integrate transactional email features, and develop more advanced lifecycle logic tied to class milestones, event participation, and in-facility purchases.

As Function Well prepares to scale into new locations, the current setup is ready to support a multi-branch model, with customisations to handle separate locations, contact owners, and location-based content.

The future is bright — and fully trackable in HubSpot.
