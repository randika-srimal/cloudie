### Challenge

As a brand-new division of Glenvill Homes, Mayde Homes needed to launch with a digital-first approach to lead capture and sales. But starting fresh didn’t mean starting simple—Mayde required a fully functional sales pipeline, distinct branding within HubSpot, and operational automations that could scale with their growth.

Their team was new to HubSpot, and time was of the essence with go-to-market plans underway. The main challenge? Creating a professional, automated, and reportable system that didn't overwhelm new users or delay rollout. They needed a platform setup that empowered their sales team to operate confidently from day one, with structured workflows and a seamless lead assignment process—no manual wrangling required.

### Solution

Our approach blended best-practice HubSpot configuration with tailored personalisation to reflect Mayde’s specific workflows and goals. The project kicked off with a Sales Discovery session, where we mapped out their pipeline stages, lead qualification criteria, and reporting needs. We built a fully customised **Sales Hub Professional** pipeline with required properties, dropdown options, and embedded logic to capture budgets, build types, and home preferences.

We also developed a branded **email template** that reflected Glenvill’s visual identity while giving Mayde its polished look. The team decided against landing pages for now but opted to convert their web forms into HubSpot-native forms, allowing for streamlined data capture and automation triggers.

Key features implemented:

* **New Business Unit configuration** to support brand separation within HubSpot.
* **Automated deal creation** and contact assignment workflows from website forms.
* **Sales sequences** with a 12-day outreach logic including SMS, calls, emails, and follow-ups.
* **Reporting dashboards** highlighting referral source tracking and lead lifecycle stages.
* **Sales team onboarding and training**, including recorded sessions and interactive demos.

We also scoped future integrations with Salesforce and Click Homes to ensure scalable expansion of their digital ecosystem.

### Results

In just six weeks, Mayde Homes evolved from concept to a fully operational digital sales engine. Their sales pipeline is now fully customised to match their unique qualification and deal-handling process. Website forms route leads instantly to the right consultant, with an automated deal created and assigned based on dropdown selections—no manual sorting or chasing required.

The custom 12-day sales sequence ensures consistent follow-up from enquiry to conversion, combining SMS, task creation, and email outreach. This structured process significantly reduces lead response time and keeps prospects engaged throughout their decision journey.

The result? A confident sales team equipped with:

* A single source of truth for all leads.
* Fast and automated lead routing.
* Real-time dashboards showing marketing source attribution, pipeline movement, and sales performance.

With 3 active sales users and full team training completed, Mayde’s new BU is ready to scale. The backend setup now allows the business to track which leads came from Glenvill referrals, digital ads, or website sources—essential data for future growth investments.

### Impact

For Mayde Homes, this transformation wasn’t just about implementing a CRM—it was about empowering a newly formed sales team with structure, visibility, and momentum. By creating a simplified, streamlined HubSpot environment, we gave their lean team the tools to move quickly, follow up confidently, and make smarter, data-informed decisions.

Post-onboarding, every customer interaction is now mapped and tracked in HubSpot, creating a seamless experience for both the team and their prospects. Lead assignment is now automated through a dropdown field, instantly creating deals and tasks for sales consultants, ensuring nothing slips through the cracks.

Managers have gained real-time insight into sales activity and pipeline health, allowing them to guide weekly focus areas with live dashboards and clear performance metrics. On the data front, we implemented custom contact properties to capture key qualification details—like budget range, land ownership, and build preferences—setting the foundation for smarter segmentation and targeted marketing campaigns down the line.

With three team inboxes fully synced and user permissions tailored to Mayde’s structure, the sales team now operates from a single source of truth. The result? Faster lead response times, better-qualified conversations, and a digital sales system that’s not just operational—it’s scalable and future-ready.
