### Challenge

As Great Place to Work expanded its global footprint, the team recognised the need to unify and streamline their marketing operations across multiple countries while preserving brand consistency. With ambitious goals for personalisation, automation, and cross-regional reporting, they sought a platform that could bring structure, scalability, and insight to their growing campaigns.

Their previous CRM setup wasn’t designed for the complexity of managing multiple domains, dynamic content, and country-specific engagement strategies. GPTW set out to enhance lead nurturing, centralise reporting, and deliver more tailored communications to their diverse audiences—without compromising operational efficiency or regional flexibility.

Data hygiene was also a key focus. A clean, structured CRM would unlock more effective segmentation and sharper campaign performance. Additionally, ensuring internal teams were fully enabled through in-depth training was critical to long-term success. This transformation wasn’t just about technology—it was about empowering global teams to operate with confidence, clarity, and a shared vision for scalable, data-led marketing.

### Solution

To address these challenges, we implemented HubSpot Marketing Hub Enterprise and Operations Hub Pro, integrating them seamlessly with Zoho CRM. We configured HubSpot’s automation tools to optimise lead nurture workflows, ensuring personalised, country-specific engagement.

We developed a robust data structure to facilitate accurate reporting and customer attribution, allowing GPTW to track the impact of marketing campaigns more effectively. Additionally, we set up dynamic footers and multiple domains to manage branding across five countries, ensuring consistency while allowing localisation.

For enhanced marketing capabilities, we designed and implemented modular email and landing page templates, enabling GPTW to create customised campaigns without reliance on external agencies. AI-powered tools were introduced for smarter engagement and predictive insights.

We also provided extensive training, including a "Train the Trainer" model, empowering internal teams to onboard new staff and maintain system efficiency. A structured data-cleaning workflow was established within the Operations Hub, ensuring ongoing data hygiene.

Lastly, a post-implementation hypercare phase was conducted, including a one-month and 90-day follow-up, to ensure a smooth transition and continued optimisation of the system.

### Results

* **Efficiency Gains:** The implementation of HubSpot Marketing Hub led to a reduction in time spent on lead nurturing, allowing the marketing team to focus on strategy rather than manual processes.
* **Improved Reporting:** With two native reporting dashboards, GPTW can now track lead conversion rates, customer journeys, and marketing attribution more effectively.
* **Enhanced Engagement:** Email campaign engagement rates increased due to the introduction of modular email templates and optimised workflows.
* **Data Hygiene & Quality:** The implementation of Operations Hub’s data-cleaning workflows reduced duplicate records by 25%, ensuring higher accuracy in customer data.
* **Independent Team Management:** Following the "Train the Trainer" approach, GPTW’s marketing teams across five countries can now manage their campaigns independently, reducing reliance on external support.

### Impact

The successful rollout of HubSpot marked a major turning point for Great Place to Work, bringing cohesion, scalability, and strategic clarity to their global marketing operations. By integrating with Zoho CRM, we established a seamless data flow between systems—streamlining processes, enhancing data accuracy, and eliminating manual tasks that previously slowed campaign execution.

Automation and personalisation are now at the heart of GPTW’s engagement strategy. With smart nurture workflows and tailored content delivery in place, the marketing team is able to connect with the right audiences at the right time—boosting campaign relevance and conversion rates. Modular templates for emails and landing pages have enabled teams to launch campaigns faster, with greater consistency across markets.

Operations Hub played a key role in establishing structured data-cleaning workflows, ensuring that contact records remain accurate and segmentation remains sharp. GPTW’s multi-country presence was also supported by dedicated brand assets and domain configurations, empowering local teams to deliver content with regional nuance while maintaining global brand integrity.

Perhaps most importantly, the project empowered GPTW’s internal teams for long-term success. Through an in-depth training program and a "Train the Trainer" enablement model, team members became confident HubSpot users—fully equipped to manage and scale campaigns independently. A structured hypercare phase ensured any post-launch adjustments were quickly addressed, further embedding platform adoption.

This transformation has positioned GPTW to operate with greater agility, insight, and alignment—equipping them to drive stronger customer engagement, scale across regions, and deliver lasting marketing impact.
