### Challenge

the lodges brand sought to streamline its marketing operations across two separate but complementary business units—the lodges brand in Australia and the hotels brand in Chile. While the company already used HubSpot Sales Hub, its adoption was limited, and it lacked the automation and reporting capabilities available in Marketing Hub Enterprise.

The need to automate urgent transactional communications and access real-time booking data from Opera and RMS was essential. Additionally, they required a way to centralize and manage marketing efforts without compromising the unique identities of the two brands. These challenges created inefficiencies, over-reliance on external agencies, and limited data visibility, which impacted their ability to scale marketing efforts effectively.

### Solution

Our team partnered with the lodges brand to implement a comprehensive HubSpot Marketing Hub Enterprise solution. Recognising the importance of operational clarity, we created two separate business units within HubSpot to manage the lodges brand and the hotels brand independently while enabling shared insights and strategies. Key elements of the solution included:

* **Automated Workflows:** We transitioned manual processes to automated workflows, including transactional emails for timely updates.
* **Custom Integrations:** Integrating HubSpot with Opera and RMS systems allowed real-time access to high-level booking data, improving reporting and data-driven decision-making.
* **Data Cleanup and Structuring:** Due to the nature of the reservations systems in place a complex data manipulation was required to split the data into relevant and actionable fields for the data migration. We cleaned and structured the data, improving accuracy and enabling the creation of active lists for personalised engagement.
* **Form replacement:** The lodges brand has a number of properties and websites, and form replacement was required for consistent data gathering and automation.
* **Lead Scoring and Lifecycle Stages:** Tailored models helped the lodges brand and the hotels brand better understand and engage their audiences.
* **Training and Documentation:** Comprehensive training empowered the internal team to manage HubSpot independently, minimising future reliance on external agencies.

### Results

The implementation yielded significant improvements in efficiency, data accuracy, and marketing performance for the lodges brand.

* **Automated transactional emails** reduced manual workload, freeing up the marketing team to focus on higher-value activities, and improving customer engagement.
* **Custom integrations** provided seamless access to booking data from Opera and RMS, enhancing reporting accuracy and real-time decision-making.
* **Advanced Segmentation:** Data was segmented on a lodge basis, allowing personalisation and a deeper connection with individual customers.
* **Tailored lead scoring and lifecycle stages** allowed for more personalized and effective engagement strategies, such as segmenting between existing customers, new leads, new customers and other key stakeholders.
* **Centralised reporting** enabled clearer insights into marketing performance, resulting in better resource allocation and increased ROI.
* **Training to support system adoption**: Most importantly, the internal teams gained the confidence and skills needed to manage and optimise HubSpot independently.

### Impact

The long-term impact of this project is evident, by automating previously manual processes, the lodges brand significantly reduced its reliance on external agencies, allowing the internal team to take full control of its marketing efforts. The integration with Opera and RMS provided new data insights, enabling better targeting, personalized communication, and more effective campaign management.

Operationally, the establishment of two distinct business units within HubSpot allowed the lodges brand and the hotels brand to maintain their unique brand identities while benefiting from shared knowledge and collaboration. Real-time data access centralised reporting, and automations managing the contact lifecycle stage, handle form submissions and segmenting data by business unit empowered the marketing team to make smarter decisions, improving response times for urgent communications and enhancing guest experiences.

In the short term, these improvements led to a noticeable increase in engagement rates and overall campaign effectiveness. In the long term, the platform is positioned to grow with the business, enabling more complex campaigns, advanced segmentation, and ongoing scalability as the brands expand.
