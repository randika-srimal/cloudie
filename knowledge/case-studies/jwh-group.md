### Challenge

With five distinct brands, each boasting its own website and audience, JWH was ready to take the next big step in unifying and scaling its customer experience. Their vision was clear: build a modern, centralised CRM solution that could support the sophistication of their marketing across all divisions—without compromising the unique identity of each brand. They sought better visibility into campaign performance, clearer lead qualification processes, and the ability to personalise and automate customer journeys at scale.

While their teams were rich in creativity and strategic ideas, they needed the right tools and infrastructure to bring these to life efficiently. By streamlining their approach to data management, reporting, and audience segmentation, JWH aimed to move beyond manual processes and lay a strong digital foundation to fuel long-term growth across the group.

### Solution

Engaging.io partnered with JWH to architect a centralised yet flexible marketing ecosystem powered by HubSpot Marketing Hub Enterprise. Each brand (Plunkett Homes, WA Country Builders, Oswald Homes, Residential Attitudes, and Rural Building Company) was set up with its own business unit in HubSpot, allowing individualised branding and reporting while maintaining oversight. We created modular email and landing page templates for each BU, enabling content teams to build assets in-house with minimal technical input.

To integrate with SugarCRM, we developed a custom-coded workflow to push qualified leads from HubSpot into their legacy CRM via a Leads Engine API. We also introduced a middleware layer to periodically sync opportunity statuses back to HubSpot, keeping the marketing team aligned with the sales journey. Reporting dashboards were built to include campaign performance, cost-per-lead, and marketing attribution across brands.

On the human side, we trained internal brand managers and marketers through a “train-the-trainer” program with supporting documentation and HubSpot Academy tracks. Workflows were created to support different customer journeys—TOF nurtures for WA Country Builders and BOF campaigns for Plunkett Homes.

### Results

The transformation was immediate. JWH now benefits from centralised reporting and automated lead nurture across all brands—something previously impossible. Lead management shifted from reactive to proactive, with tailored workflows that warm leads over 3 months to 3 years depending on buying stage.

The integration bridged SugarCRM’s legacy data with HubSpot’s advanced capabilities, meaning Marketing now has visibility over lead status and conversion opportunities. Email and landing page development time was slashed with modular templates, and time-consuming manual report generation has been replaced by live dashboards tracking lead sources, CPL, and engagement metrics.

Early signs show campaign attribution clarity and a dramatic reduction in time spent managing lists and forms across brands. HubSpot’s connection with Google and Meta Ads also gives their digital team new superpowers in optimising spend and performance.

### Impact

In just 12 weeks, JWH laid the groundwork for a more connected, scalable future. By transitioning to a unified CRM, the group enabled each brand to maintain its unique voice while unlocking the efficiencies of shared infrastructure and enterprise-level automation. Marketing teams across the business now have the autonomy and agility to launch campaigns, build forms, and track performance—without relying on developer support.

The new system has strengthened the alignment between sales and marketing, introducing more intelligent lead segmentation and paving the way for a RevOps strategy in upcoming phases. With lifecycle stages clearly defined and customer journeys automated, JWH now operates from a single source of truth—making decision-making faster and more data-driven.

This transformation has done more than improve operations; it’s created a scalable model for future growth. With a blueprint now in place, JWH is well-positioned to extend its digital transformation into sales and service, enabling deeper personalisation, richer reporting, and more cohesive cross-brand collaboration.
