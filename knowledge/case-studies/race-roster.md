### Challenge (150 words)

Race Roster faced multiple challenges with Salesforce, including high costs, complexity, and limited customization for evolving business needs. The growing volume of customer data had become disorganized, making it difficult to track leads and manage customer interactions effectively. Without a defined customer journey, Race Roster struggled to align their sales and marketing teams and lacked visibility into key metrics.

Before implementing HubSpot, a thorough data cleanup was required. Additionally, Race Roster needed to establish an organized CRM structure and automate workflows to improve efficiency and lead management.

### Solution (250 words)

We implemented HubSpot’s Marketing Hub, Sales Hub, and Service Hub to create a comprehensive CRM solution. Data from Salesforce was cleaned and migrated into HubSpot, creating a fresh foundation.

Marketing Hub provided the tools to define Race Roster’s customer journey and establish two email templates and landing pages for ongoing use. Sales Hub streamlined lead management with four custom deal pipelines and 60 automated workflows, simplifying lead assignment and nurturing processes.

Service Hub introduced an omni-channel support experience, integrating tickets from seven inboxes into three pipelines. This restructured support model allowed customer service representatives to focus on renewals, improving collaboration between departments. Automated onboarding workflows ensured new customers received proper support.

HubSpot’s reporting capabilities enabled Race Roster to track contacts, deals, and companies across parent-child relationships, improving visibility into business operations.

### Results (250 words)

The transition to HubSpot led to significant improvements for Race Roster:

* **Data Management**: Organized customer data, making it easily accessible and actionable.
* **Workflow Automation**: Over 60 workflows streamlined lead management, reducing manual effort and improving efficiency.
* **Support System Upgrade**: The Service Hub implementation allowed Race Roster to categorize tickets and provide a better customer experience.
* **Sales Efficiency**: Custom pipelines and automation enhanced the sales process, helping the team close deals faster.
* **Advanced Reporting**: HubSpot’s native reporting features provided deep insights into business performance, especially for managing large-scale events like Iron Man.

### Impact (300 words)

Adopting HubSpot transformed Race Roster’s business operations. Centralizing customer data and creating automated workflows allowed their teams to collaborate more effectively. The sales team had clear processes and the tools needed to close deals faster, while the marketing team could execute campaigns with a well-defined customer journey in place.

Service Hub enhanced customer support by categorizing inquiries and streamlining the renewal process. Automated workflows kept customer onboarding on track, reducing the risk of missed steps. HubSpot’s reporting capabilities provided Race Roster with deeper insights into its processes, enabling data-driven decisions that were previously difficult to achieve with Salesforce.

Overall, the transition to HubSpot improved operational efficiency boosted productivity across teams, and allowed Race Roster to focus on delivering exceptional service to its clients.
