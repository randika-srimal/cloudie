### Challenge

the global property data and analytics business set out to modernise their marketing capabilities with a unified platform that could support advanced personalisation, robust reporting, and streamlined campaign execution across global teams. With operations spanning both the US and Australia, the business needed to replace its legacy marketing automation platform with a solution that could scale efficiently—without sacrificing regional nuance or data integrity.

Their goal was to align two distinct teams—each with unique needs—under a single marketing ecosystem. The US required a custom-built integration with guardrails to preserve Salesforce as the source of truth. Layered on top of this was the need for consistent data governance, enhanced visibility across regions, and a compressed delivery timeline ahead of the holiday period and expiration of the previous platform.

This wasn’t just a platform migration; it was a cross-functional, cross-time-zone transformation. The global property data and analytics business needed a partner who could handle the technical complexity while helping both teams work toward a shared vision for scalable, insight-driven marketing operations.

### Solution

To deliver a streamlined marketing solution across regions, Engaging.io implemented a phased onboarding strategy that aligned technology, people, and processes. We ran discovery workshops across both business units to map marketing processes and define requirements for HubSpot setup.

For the **AU team**, we implemented complex forms and automations processes to support their specific resource downloading.

For the **US team**, we scoped and built a **custom integration layer**, including middleware development to manage complex data sync logic, avoid record duplication, and enforce a strict one-way sync for specific objects. This ensured data integrity, aligned systems, and reduced manual data intervention across teams.

Core configuration included:

* **Marketing Hub Enterprise setup** with email and landing page templates
* **Operations Hub Professional** for custom-coded workflows and data formatting
* **Subscription center setup** and preference management for both teams
* **Lead scoring, persona tagging**, and form automation
* **Six dashboards** tailored to marketing KPIs across both BUs
* **Modular design systems** for ongoing marketing independence

We executed a **train-the-trainer program**, arming key team members with the tools, knowledge, and documentation needed to maintain and scale the platform independently. Finally, we supported IP warm-up and completed all necessary data migration, including a manual import of unsubscribes from Pardot to maintain compliance.

### Results

By project completion, both the global property data and analytics business US and AU teams were fully operational in HubSpot with distinct but aligned operations. Marketing teams were trained and empowered to manage campaigns, data, and reporting with minimal reliance on external vendors.

Key results included:

* **100% reduction in marketing platform fragmentation** by unifying on HubSpot
* **Faster campaign execution**, with modular templates reducing build time  significantly
* **Custom dashboards** gave leadership real-time visibility into key KPIs
* **Improved email deliverability** through IP warm-up and preference center adoption
* **Seamless data sync** between HubSpot and Salesforce for US, reducing record duplication and confusion

The decision to implement AU and US simultaneously saved resources, streamlined change management, and ensured process consistency. The global property data and analytics business now benefits from one centralised marketing platform that supports regional autonomy while promoting global alignment.

### Impact

The HubSpot implementation marked an exciting leap forward for the global property data and analytics business’s global marketing operations. By consolidating systems into a single, flexible platform, the business unlocked a new level of agility across both US and AU teams—empowering them to manage segmentation, campaign execution, and personalisation with confidence and speed.

With a carefully planned integration strategy, Salesforce continues to operate as the trusted source of truth, while HubSpot now acts as a powerful engagement layer. Our tailored approach ensured that each regional team’s needs were met, all while maintaining data integrity and eliminating past sync challenges.

The introduction of modular email templates, a centralised subscription center, and real-time dashboards has streamlined processes and boosted visibility across marketing and leadership teams. These tools have made it easier to execute campaigns, stay compliant, and monitor performance—all without adding complexity to internal workflows.

Through hands-on training, documentation, and access to HubSpot Academy resources, the global property data and analytics business’s marketing team now operates with greater independence and strategic clarity. The result is not just faster execution, but a platform and team ready to scale, adapt, and lead with data as they continue to grow globally.
