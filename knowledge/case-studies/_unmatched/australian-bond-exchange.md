### Challenge

Australian Bond Exchange (ABE) has always had a strong vision for delivering best-in-class service to investors—and with their business rapidly scaling across both retail and institutional channels, they recognised it was time to evolve their systems to match.

The team was already leveraging a range of tools like Mautic, Google Ads, and custom spreadsheets to manage lead engagement and compliance workflows, but they saw an opportunity to centralise and streamline. With growing volumes of client interactions, expanding product offerings, and high regulatory standards, ABE proactively sought a platform that could bring all channels together and create a single source of truth—without compromising on compliance or user experience.

They had a clear understanding of what they needed: greater automation, stronger visibility into client status (such as KYC and trading readiness), simplified campaign execution, and tighter alignment between Sales and Marketing teams. Rather than continuing with manual workarounds or incremental fixes, ABE made the strategic decision to invest in a CRM solution that could scale with them—enabling smarter engagement and operational efficiency across the entire customer journey.

### Solution

To support Australian Bond Exchange’s strategic growth and operational needs, Engaging.io delivered a multi-layered HubSpot implementation designed around compliance, scalability, and high-touch customer engagement. The project began with a series of discovery workshops across Sales, Marketing, and Compliance teams, where we mapped core processes—such as investor onboarding, advisor engagement, lead-to-trade progression, and referral workflows—into actionable CRM architecture.

Our approach was rooted in leveraging the full capability of HubSpot Marketing Hub Enterprise and Sales Hub Professional, while ensuring the system was intuitive for day-to-day users and sophisticated enough to support regulatory and business complexities.

**Key solution elements included:**

#### **🔁 Marketing Automation Overhaul**

We replaced ABE’s existing marketing automation platform (Mautic) with a cleaner, more powerful setup in Marketing Hub Enterprise. This included:

* Rebuilding all nurture sequences with smart segmentation logic based on investor type (e.g. retail, advisor, SMSF).
* Creating subscription types for granular opt-in tracking (e.g. newsletters, bond offers, event invites).
* Developing smart content and dynamic email templates to personalise messaging at scale.
* Implementing 5+ nurture workflows, including post-event sequences, onboarding follow-ups, and re-engagement campaigns.
* Connecting HubSpot with Facebook, Google, and Bing Ads to track campaign performance and conversions directly within HubSpot reporting dashboards.

#### **🧩 Custom CRM Card for Compliance Data**

Given ABE’s regulatory obligations, we developed a **custom CRM card** embedded within HubSpot contact records. This surfaced:

* Real-time KYC/AML verification data from ABE’s internal systems and Australia Post
* Recent submission summaries
* Secure document access via session-based iFrame tokens
  This ensured sales reps could see compliance status without needing access to sensitive data storage systems—keeping ABE both secure and efficient.

#### **🔐 OAuth-Secured Middleware Integration**

We built a secure middleware layer that handled token-based authentication between HubSpot and ABE’s external systems. Key features included:

* OAuth 2.0 authentication
* Time-limited token creation and expiry
* Validation workflows to confirm access rights on each session
  This allowed the CRM card to display private customer data only when a HubSpot user had appropriate permissions, without storing the data inside HubSpot—an essential step to meet compliance requirements.

#### **🔄 Sales Pipeline Redesign**

We created multiple custom sales pipelines in Sales Hub Professional to align with ABE’s hybrid business model:

* One for B2C retail clients
* One for financial advisors and planners (B2B)
* A referral and onboarding pipeline for advisor-driven lead sources
  Each pipeline included custom deal stages, lead scoring rules, and automated triggers based on contact behaviour and lifecycle progression. Features like lead assignment automation, custom properties for trade readiness, and association labels were configured to provide clarity between individuals, companies, and referred contacts.

#### **📊 Operational Dashboards & Reporting**

To support data-driven decisions across the business, we built:

* Marketing ROI dashboards segmented by channel, campaign, and subscription type
* Sales dashboards filtered by region, team member, and lifecycle stage
* Deal tracking for soft and hard bond orders, including maturing bond alerts
* Visibility into coupon payments and offer follow-ups, tracked using custom properties

#### **👩‍🏫 Training & Enablement**

Our implementation followed a **train-the-trainer** model:

* Conducted tailored group sessions for Sales and Marketing teams
* Provided recorded tutorials, cheat sheets, and CRM usage guides
* Recommended relevant HubSpot Academy courses to deepen internal capability
* Configured user roles and permissions to support a self-managed environment post-handover

Through the use of native HubSpot tools—workflows, custom properties, custom objects, smart content, and integrations—as well as a tailored technical layer, we built a solution that didn't just fit ABE’s business model but actively enhanced it. By the end of the implementation, teams were enabled, compliant data was accessible securely, and both campaign and pipeline management were running with far greater efficiency and accuracy.

### Results

The results of the HubSpot implementation were both immediate and strategic—transforming how ABE operates across Marketing, Sales, and Compliance. By consolidating their fragmented systems and automating previously manual workflows, the team gained clarity, efficiency, and confidence in day-to-day operations.

One of the biggest gains was in process efficiency. Email campaigns that once required hours of coordination between systems can now be launched in minutes using modular, pre-approved templates. This has resulted in an overall reduction in campaign build time, freeing the Marketing team to focus on strategic growth activities rather than admin.

Lead scoring and smart segmentation are now automatically determining who is trading-ready versus who requires further nurturing. This means Sales teams are no longer manually flagging clients for follow-up—they're receiving prioritised, actionable lists daily, ensuring timely and relevant contact. In the first three months post-implementation, ABE saw an increase in lead-to-trade conversion velocity, with more consistent engagement across cohorts.

Crucially, the integration with Australia Post and the custom CRM card introduced real-time KYC status visibility without compromising compliance. Advisors and sales consultants can see whether a client has completed identity verification, accelerating onboarding while removing the need to navigate external systems or wait for compliance updates. This eliminated several manual steps from the onboarding process and reduced lead conversion delays by up to 48 hours per client.

Sales consultants are also now better informed. HubSpot’s timeline activity and marketing email visibility features allow Sales to see what content a client has received, opened, or clicked—enabling proactive and relevant conversations. Consultants can also monitor bond maturity timelines, thanks to automated alerts and workflows, which has opened up more cross-sell and reinvestment opportunities earlier in the client lifecycle.

Perhaps most notably, the implementation has enabled ABE to maintain strict compliance protocols while also increasing agility. Sensitive data remains securely stored outside of HubSpot, yet the relevant team members can access it via the secure CRM card interface—preserving both privacy and productivity.

In summary, ABE is no longer constrained by a patchwork of tools and workarounds. With HubSpot as their central platform, they are operating with greater speed, clarity, and confidence. The result? A scalable, compliant, and insight-driven system that’s powering smarter decisions and stronger client relationships.

### Impact

ABE now has a fully scalable CRM system that supports its investor lifecycle from onboarding to bond maturity. The integration of KYC visibility through the CRM card has improved operational transparency while safeguarding compliance protocols.

Marketing teams can launch, manage, and report on campaigns across investor segments in real-time, without developer input. Sales consultants now access insights at the contact level, helping them prioritise follow-ups, manage maturing bond offers, and pre-empt customer needs.

In the long term, the custom architecture has positioned ABE to scale efficiently. New advisors, clients, or jurisdictions can be added with minor configuration, not full rebuilds. The move from manual spreadsheets and disjointed platforms to a centralised, intelligent system marks a fundamental shift—not just in tooling, but in capability.
