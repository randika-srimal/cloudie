##  EXTERNAL – BLOG CASE STUDY CONTENT

Metadata (≤155 characters)
How Montu unified Sales and Service in HubSpot Enterprise to scale operations and support rapid HealthTech growth.

AI Narration (1:30–4 mins spoken)

Montu is one of Australia’s fastest-growing HealthTech companies, operating at the intersection of telehealth, pharmaceutical distribution and medical technology. With rapid growth came operational complexity — and their CRM needed to evolve just as quickly.

Montu was running sales and service through Zendesk Sell and Zendesk Service. While functional, the systems were siloed. Reporting was fragmented. Automations were limited. For a business managing complex B2B prescriber relationships and high-volume patient interactions, that created friction.

The business had already mapped its processes extensively in Miro — from deal stages and compliance checkpoints to prescriber segmentation and handovers. The challenge wasn’t defining the process. It was translating that into a scalable, enterprise-grade HubSpot configuration within a tight nine-week window.

We began by reviewing and optimising their documentation. Rather than replicating a tag-heavy structure, we streamlined the architecture using HubSpot-native properties, views and reporting logic. This reduced automation overhead and long-term maintenance risk.

Sales Hub Enterprise was configured with a structured deal pipeline, territory-based assignment logic, lead scoring and routing. Service Hub Enterprise was implemented with a consolidated ticket pipeline, SLA configuration and service reporting dashboards.

A critical component was the AHPRA number — the unique identifier used across Montu’s internal systems. We designed an integration framework around that identifier to support duplicate management, prescriber segmentation and future automation capabilities.

Within nine weeks, Montu transitioned from fragmented systems to a unified HubSpot Enterprise platform. The result wasn’t just a new CRM — it was operational clarity, structured automation and a scalable foundation designed for continued growth.

Montu is Australia’s largest medical cannabis company, operating a fully integrated ecosystem spanning telehealth, pharmacy distribution and clinician education. As the organisation scaled rapidly, they required a CRM platform capable of supporting complex workflows, regulatory requirements and advanced reporting.

**The Problem**

Scaling Fast in a Regulated Industry

Montu’s growth demanded more than a basic CRM. They needed structured visibility, automation and system cohesion.

Problem 1 – Fragmented Systems and Reporting
Running sales and service through separate Zendesk platforms limited cross-team visibility and forecasting accuracy. Reporting lacked centralisation and scalability.

Problem 2 – Complex Processes Not Designed for Automation
Montu’s prescriber pathways, territory ownership and AHPRA-linked workflows were too sophisticated for their existing setup to automate effectively at scale.

• Sales and service dashboards

**The Implementation**

With a defined nine-week timeline, execution had to be disciplined and outcome-driven from the outset. We began by reviewing and optimising Montu’s detailed Miro documentation, pressure-testing the proposed processes against HubSpot Enterprise best practice. Rather than replicating complexity, we streamlined the architecture to reduce automation overhead, simplify data structures and ensure long-term scalability before configuration began.

Sales Hub Enterprise was built around a structured deal pipeline aligned to Montu’s prescriber journey, with territory-based assignment logic automating ownership and reducing manual allocation. Lead scoring and routing were layered in to prioritise opportunities and improve forecasting visibility. In Service Hub Enterprise, previously fragmented workflows were consolidated into a single ticket pipeline with SLA configuration and automation to create measurable, predictable service performance.

A key technical consideration was Montu’s use of the AHPRA number as a unique identifier across internal systems. We designed the CRM data structure to support this lookup logic, enabling duplicate prevention and future integration capability. Weekly WIP sessions ensured commercial alignment throughout the build, while reporting dashboards were configured in parallel to embed leadership visibility from day one.

The project concluded with structured Train-the-Trainer sessions, governance setup and defined user permissions to support compliance and adoption. By go-live, Montu had not only transitioned to HubSpot Enterprise — they had established a scalable, operationally aligned CRM foundation built to support continued growth.

**Impact and Results**

Immediate Operational Clarity

Within nine weeks, Montu transitioned to a unified HubSpot Enterprise environment. Sales gained structured ownership and automated routing. Service teams worked within a consolidated pipeline supported by SLA tracking.

Leadership gained improved forecasting visibility and centralised reporting across prescriber and service workflows.

Long-Term Scalability

By simplifying data structures and leveraging HubSpot-native functionality, Montu reduced future maintenance complexity. The AHPRA-based architecture positioned the business for continued automation maturity.

Most importantly, Montu now operates on a CRM foundation built for scale — supporting rapid growth while maintaining structure, visibility and control.
