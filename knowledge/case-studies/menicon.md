## **Client Overview**

Menicon Co., Ltd. is a global leader in contact lens design, production, and eye care innovation. With operations spanning Asia, Europe, and North America, Menicon’s digital ecosystem had grown fragmented over time, with multiple regional websites and inconsistent user experiences. In 2023, Menicon initiated a strategic digital transformation project to unify its global online presence and professional education platforms. This case study won an award in the HubSpot impact Awards for Product Excellence in 2025
---

## **Challenge**

### **Fragmented Digital Presence**

Menicon’s web infrastructure consisted of two separate systems for Global/Asia and US/Europe. These systems, launched three years prior, lacked integration and consistency. The company identified several pain points:

* Inconsistent visual identity and user experience across regions.
* High maintenance costs and duplicated content management efforts.
* Limited CMS functionality for regional teams.
* Insufficient marketing tools and analytics for audience engagement.
* A disconnected Academy platform that could not easily support e-learning expansion

### **Strategic Goals**

Menicon’s digital transformation objectives were to:

* Unify global and regional websites into a single CMS environment.
* Enhance visual consistency through new design systems.
* Enable regional autonomy while maintaining brand coherence.
* Integrate the Menicon Academy learning platform with existing systems like EasyFit.
* Improve marketing agility through better analytics, SEO tools, and CRM connectivity.

## **Solution**

### **Platform Selection**

After a competitive selection process, Menicon chose Engaging.io to lead the project. HubSpot CMS was selected as the foundation due to its flexibility, native integrations, and ability to handle multi-regional, multi-language deployments

The solution encompassed:

1. Global and regional website network built within HubSpot CMS.
2. Menicon Academy member portal, integrated with Easygenerator or LearnUpon.
3. Auth0 single sign-on (SSO) for EasyFit users, ensuring seamless cross-platform access.

## **Project Scope**

### **1. Global Website Network**

Engaging.io developed a comprehensive HubSpot-based website system supporting:

* Multiple brands and markets, with templates and content modules for both corporate and consumer-facing sites.
* Two complete design systems—*Origami* and *Mizuhiki*—to reflect Menicon’s Japanese heritage and contemporary global identity

* Multilingual capability, using HubSpot’s language variants.
* Flexible CMS editing, enabling regional marketing teams to manage their own sites, pages, and media.
* Advanced marketing features, such as SEO fields, analytics integrations (GA, GSC, GTM, Matomo), and social sharing.

### **2. Menicon Academy Portal**

A new membership-based learning platform was built within HubSpot CMS, featuring:

* Gated content and manual registration approval.
* Role-based access management.
* Dynamic content display based on user profiles.
* Integration options for Easygenerator and LearnUpon to deliver e-learning content.
* Centralized SSO using Auth0, allowing EasyFit users to log in automatically

### **3. Design and Functionality Enhancements**

Both platforms shared a unified modular system for:

* Post filtering for archives (products, jobs, courses).
* ECP/store locator with filtering and bulk upload.
* Newsletter subscriptions and GDPR compliance tools.
* Comprehensive backend capabilities, including custom code areas, header/footer editors, and API integration for content sync across sites.
