## **🧵 Summary of the Business**

Cairns Adventure Group manages a portfolio of adventure tourism brands, each with unique offerings but shared operational complexity. As the business continued to grow, they partnered with Engaging.io to unify their systems, improve internal visibility, and create a more scalable, connected experience for both teams and customers.

---

## **🚧 The Problem**

### **Levelling Up Systems to Support Growth**

As Cairns Adventure Group expanded across brands and markets, they identified an opportunity to elevate internal efficiencies and customer experience by unifying their systems. Their goals were clear: improve coordination across departments, streamline how group bookings and agent relationships were managed, and give internal teams greater visibility into the full customer journey. While their existing tools supported day-to-day operations, they were ready to evolve into a more connected, scalable and data-driven model.

**Problem 1 – Siloed Booking Workflows Limiting Visibility**
Cairns Adventure Group managed a high volume of group and charter bookings across multiple brands, each with its own unique requirements. While their booking system handled transactions effectively, the broader workflow — including quoting, approvals, and operational handover — lacked centralised visibility. This made it more challenging to track booking progress, flag special conditions, and ensure timely communication across teams.

**Problem 2 – Agent Engagement and Onboarding Wasn’t Scalable**
With a large network of domestic and international agents, CAG wanted to strengthen how they onboarded, engaged, and supported these relationships. Existing processes relied on a mix of tools and manual tracking, which made it difficult to consistently nurture agents, trigger follow-ups, or segment them effectively based on performance and activity.

---

## **✅ The Solution**

As Cairns Adventure Group looked to scale across brands and regions, they recognised the need for a more connected, customer-centric platform that could align internal teams and enhance efficiency. Engaging.io designed a tailored HubSpot ecosystem to bring their group bookings, agent relationships, and customer service operations into a unified digital framework — built for scalability, visibility, and long-term growth.

---

## **🧠 The Strategic Plan**

### **📋 Connected Deal Pipelines for Bookings and Sales**

* Built a dedicated **Group & Charter pipeline** to manage inquiries, quotes, confirmations, and follow-ups.
* Automated key steps such as notifications and task assignments to ensure smooth operational handovers.
* Created a **centralised Agent pipeline** to track onboarding, engagement, and reactivation opportunities.

### **🔗 Custom Integration with Respax**

* Developed a **custom API integration** to sync booking data into HubSpot.
* Enabled visibility of reservation details, guest numbers, and booking status within deal records.
* Supported near real-time updates to keep teams aligned on the latest booking information.

### **🤝 Scalable Agent Onboarding & Nurture**

* Implemented a fully digital **agent onboarding journey** using HubSpot forms and workflows.
* Automated segmentation based on agent type, location, and activity level.
* Introduced nurture workflows to support onboarding, engagement, and ongoing communication.

### **🧾 Streamlined Internal Service Workflows**

* Rebuilt complaint, refund, and amendment processes using **Service Hub pipelines**.
* Introduced automated routing, task assignment, and tracking for internal requests.
* Improved visibility and accountability across customer service teams.

### **🎨 Multi-Brand Marketing Enablement**

* Designed modular **email and landing page templates** for all CAG brands.
* Enabled the marketing team to launch campaigns quickly and independently.
* Centralised audience segmentation and communication preferences.

---

## **🔧 The Implementation**

The implementation focused on aligning HubSpot with CAG’s real-world workflows while ensuring long-term usability across teams.

* Custom pipelines were configured to reflect group booking and agent lifecycle stages.
* Forms were redesigned with conditional logic to reduce data duplication and improve accuracy.
* Booking data from Respax was mapped into HubSpot deal records for full visibility.
* Service Hub pipelines were implemented to manage internal requests consistently.
* Dashboards and reporting were created to provide insight into bookings, agent performance, and operational activity.
* Training sessions were delivered across sales, marketing, and customer service teams to support adoption and confidence.

---

## **📈 Impact and Results**

Cairns Adventure Group now operates on a unified platform that connects sales, operations, and customer service, creating a more seamless experience for both teams and customers.

Group bookings are easier to manage, with clear visibility across each stage and automated workflows supporting coordination between teams. Key information such as special conditions and booking details is surfaced at the right time, helping ensure a consistent and well-prepared customer experience.

Agent onboarding and engagement are now structured and scalable, with automated journeys supporting new agents and ongoing communication tailored to activity and performance. This has enabled the sales team to focus on relationship-building rather than administrative tasks.

Customer service processes have also been enhanced, with internal requests now tracked, assigned, and managed within HubSpot. This has improved response times, accountability, and overall visibility across the team.

With these foundations in place, Cairns Adventure Group is well positioned to scale — with systems that support growth, improve efficiency, and deliver a consistently high-quality experience across every touchpoint.
