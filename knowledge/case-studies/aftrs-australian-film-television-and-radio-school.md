### Challenge

AFTRS had already taken strong steps toward building a connected marketing and CRM ecosystem by adopting both HubSpot and Salesforce. With a growing database of over 40,000 marketing contacts, their team recognised the importance of refining their tech stack to better support their strategic goals—particularly around consent management, data integrity, and audience segmentation.

The team was forward-thinking in identifying several key areas for improvement. They wanted to ensure that every contact’s preferences were honoured, that data flowed seamlessly between systems, and that internal teams had clarity around campaign performance and contact status. Subscription workflows needed to be more accurate, and platform syncing logic—especially around Salesforce custom objects like ‘Courses’ and ‘AFTRS Qualifications’—required reconfiguration to support streamlined operations.

Rather than continuing with workarounds, AFTRS proactively partnered with Engaging.io to resolve these challenges. Their aim was clear: to create a solid, scalable foundation for their marketing operations that could deliver meaningful engagement while maintaining compliance and clarity across departments.

### Solution

From the very beginning, the AFTRS team approached this project with clarity, collaboration, and a genuine commitment to doing things right. Working together, we kicked off with a thorough integration health check and a series of hands-on workshops that brought Marketing, IT, and CRM stakeholders in the same room—aligning on both goals and practical outcomes.

With a 60-hour support retainer in place, we took a phased and purposeful approach, carefully untangling the pain points and rebuilding the system with a smarter, more sustainable foundation.

Here’s how we did it:

* Smarter Syncing Rules: We redesigned the Salesforce-to-HubSpot integration from the ground up—ensuring Salesforce remained the source of truth, while bi-directional syncing was only applied where truly needed (like managing email preferences). This dramatically reduced data conflicts and duplication issues.
* Clean Inclusion List Logic: We reimagined the logic behind AFTRS’ sync inclusion lists, helping the team confidently control which contacts should sync—and when. This gave both Marketing and IT teams better visibility and reduced system noise across platforms.
* Resubscription with Consent: Subscription management was overhauled using HubSpot’s GDPR-friendly preferences. We introduced a new resubscription form that gave contacts a clear, transparent way to manage their preferences—reducing friction and aligning with AFTRS’ commitment to consent-based marketing.
* Custom Object Clarity: We helped the team evaluate which Salesforce objects were truly needed and scoped a cleaner, more relevant object strategy. This included recommending a future sync of ‘Course Connections’ to power things like abandoned cart journeys and personalised course offers.
* Safe, Controlled Testing: To ensure changes didn’t disrupt live operations, we set up a dedicated HubSpot Sandbox connected to Salesforce’s test environment—allowing AFTRS to safely validate sync behaviour and workflow logic before anything went live.

At every step, this project was about empowerment—giving the AFTRS team not just cleaner data and more reliable systems, but the confidence to grow, communicate, and campaign without compromise.

### Results

Within three months, AFTRS significantly reduced sync errors and prevented thousands of unwanted contact records from being pushed into Salesforce. Subscription form accuracy improved, allowing contacts to manage preferences seamlessly and in compliance with consent standards. Email list hygiene improved, with an uplift in deliverability and a reduction in unsubscribes across key campaign types.

Marketing and IT now operate with shared clarity. AFTRS can confidently launch campaigns knowing contacts will flow correctly between systems, and Salesforce data integrity is maintained. Duplicate contacts have been reduced, integration logic simplified, and contact visibility between platforms restored.

### Impact

The impact of this project has been wide-reaching across AFTRS’ operational and strategic teams. Where once marketing automation was reactive and constrained by integration pitfalls, it’s now consistent, compliant, and scalable.

Salesforce is now protected from being cluttered with marketing-only contacts, and HubSpot serves as a powerful front-end for campaign activity. Consent is tracked with precision, supporting AFTRS’ commitment to transparency and trust.

Marketing campaigns are launched faster and with greater confidence. Contact records are complete, subscription logic is sound, and user journeys can now be tracked and refined without cross-platform conflict.

The AFTRS team—particularly across Marketing, CRM and IT—now collaborate more effectively, grounded in a shared system architecture that supports both compliance and creativity.
