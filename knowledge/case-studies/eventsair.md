### Challenge

EventsAir faced significant challenges due to disconnected systems that hindered their ability to make data-driven decisions and create personalised customer experiences.

Their reliance on Zoho for CRM and finance, combined with Outreach for outbound marketing, required comprehensive integrations to ensure a unified customer view. While these systems provided valuable functionality, managing data across multiple platforms introduced complexities, highlighting opportunities to enhance automation and streamline processes for greater efficiency.

### Solution

To address these challenges, we implemented HubSpot Marketing Hub Enterprise as the central platform for all marketing activities. Our team focused on:

* **Seamless Integrations**: We integrated HubSpot with Outreach and Zoho to establish a continuous flow of real-time data, providing a unified view of customer interactions.
* **Automated Workflows**: Custom automated workflows were developed to improve lead management, email marketing, and customer engagement. These workflows reduced manual efforts and ensured timely follow-ups.
* **Lifecycle Management**: EventsAir needed HubSpot to work seamlessly with their existing platforms, which meant lifecycle triggers were essential to get right. To do this we implemented integration with two-way sync between Zoho and HubSpot, allowing a seamless handoff at each stage and keeping the workflows and automation intact.
* **Custom Reporting Dashboards**: We set up dashboards to track key marketing metrics, such as email engagement, conversion rates, and campaign ROI, allowing the team to make informed decisions.
* **Training and Documentation**: Comprehensive training sessions equipped the EventsAir marketing team with the knowledge to manage HubSpot independently. Documentation provided ongoing support and reference materials.

### Results

The transformation was immediate and impactful. Key outcomes included:

* **Efficiency Gains**: Automated workflows saved significant time for the marketing team, allowing them to focus on strategic initiatives.
* **Improved Campaign Performance**: Personalised communications resulted in increased email engagement and higher conversion rates.
* **Enhanced Data Consistency**: Integration with Outreach and Zoho reduced data silos, creating a single source of truth for customer information.
* **Data-Driven Decisions**: Custom reporting dashboards offered real-time insights, enabling continuous improvement in marketing strategies.

### Impact

The implementation of HubSpot Marketing Hub Professional revolutionized EventsAir’s marketing operations. With automated workflows and integrated systems, the marketing team no longer spent countless hours on manual tasks but could dedicate more time to optimising campaigns and engaging with prospects.

The unified customer view facilitated smoother collaboration between the marketing and sales teams, ensuring that prospects received timely and relevant communication at every stage of their journey.

EventsAir’s marketing strategy became more agile, with real-time reporting offering immediate feedback on campaign performance. The tailored training sessions empowered their team to confidently manage the platform and explore new features to enhance their marketing efforts continuously. In the short term, efficiency gains and improved campaign performance were evident. In the long term, the foundation was set for scalable growth and innovation in their marketing initiatives.
