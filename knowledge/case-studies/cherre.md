### Challenge (150 words)

Cherre had less than three months to transition from Pardot, their outdated marketing platform, to HubSpot. The lack of integration between Pardot and Salesforce caused delays in lead flow, misaligned marketing and sales, and resulted in poor performance for emails and landing pages. Additionally, Pardot’s limited reporting capabilities hindered management’s ability to make data-driven decisions.

### Solution (250 words)

The first step was creating a seamless integration between HubSpot and Salesforce, ensuring data synchronization for contacts, companies, deals, and activities. The team then built custom customer journeys and a tailored lead-scoring model to enhance lead quality and guide prospects through the sales funnel. Active lists in HubSpot were implemented to dynamically manage contacts.

Finally, reporting dashboards were set up to track attribution and provide management with the insights needed to monitor campaign performance and make strategic decisions. This transformation aligned marketing and sales, improved asset performance, and optimized workflows.

### Results (250 words)

Adding HubSpot to Cherre’s tech stack resulted in significant improvements. The marketing team gained the tools to create more engaging content and automate workflows, freeing up time for strategic projects. The sales team benefited from high-quality leads driven by the new lead scoring model, while management enjoyed full visibility into performance metrics. These changes empowered data-driven decision-making and provided a scalable foundation for future growth.

### Impact (300 words)

Cherre’s migration to HubSpot improved not only its marketing and sales processes but also its overall operational efficiency. By automating lead scoring and data integration, Cherre could focus on high-impact activities instead of manual processes. The realignment between marketing and sales fostered collaboration, allowing both teams to work towards shared goals with actionable data.

The lead scoring model ensured sales teams targeted the most promising leads, shortening the sales cycle and boosting conversion rates. Meanwhile, the marketing team had access to powerful tools for personalized outreach and detailed campaign tracking. Management gained access to real-time data and reporting, which improved resource allocation and strategic planning.

Overall, Cherre transitioned from a fragmented system to a fully integrated solution, resulting in greater agility and adaptability to market changes.
