### Challenge

the Australian air conditioning and ventilation manufacturer set out to streamline and modernise their service operations by bringing together fragmented systems into one unified, scalable platform. With support functions previously spread across multiple tools—centred around Zendesk—there was a clear opportunity to enhance efficiency, clarity, and collaboration across teams.

The company’s service experience was ready for a transformation. Their goal was to simplify the customer journey, improve ticket routing, and provide a seamless experience for both internal teams and external users such as installers and customers. A major step in that journey involved consolidating their separate HubSpot portals—one for Service and one for Sales/Marketing—into a single, cohesive environment.

With a fixed deadline due to Zendesk licence expiry, the project required a clean, well-structured cutover that could be executed quickly and confidently. Rather than migrating legacy data, the Australian air conditioning and ventilation manufacturer focused on a fresh start—rebuilding their service infrastructure from the ground up to support long-term efficiency, visibility, and customer satisfaction.

### Solution

We kicked off with a comprehensive discovery and download session, mapping out the Australian air conditioning and ventilation manufacturer’s current Zendesk workflows and ticket types across business units. With no legacy data to migrate, we built a clean Service Hub instance within their main HubSpot portal. Four ticket pipelines were configured: Installer Warranty, Homeowner Support, ERP/Internal, and Sales Support. Each pipeline was built with automated stage transitions, CSAT survey triggers, and SLA-based notifications to escalate stagnant tickets.

Forms from the Zendesk portal were rebuilt using HubSpot’s native tools and linked to their relevant pipelines. A chatbot was implemented to assist users in surfacing relevant support content or routing queries based on issue type and geography. We also built a unified Knowledge Base and connected it to the chatbot for better self-service.

Training was conducted via a “train the trainer” model, empowering internal champions with recordings, cheat sheets, and walk-throughs. We provided dashboard reporting, SLA insights, and service forecasting tools for visibility across departments.

Finally, advisory sessions were used to audit their existing Sales and Marketing Hubs, laying the groundwork for optimisation.

### Results (250 words)

The result? A single, centralised HubSpot platform that now supports the Australian air conditioning and ventilation manufacturer’s end-to-end customer service process with speed, structure, and scale.

The Australian air conditioning and ventilation manufacturer’s support teams can now manage tickets with confidence thanks to clearly defined pipelines, automated alerts, and real-time reporting. CSAT feedback is now automatically triggered on ticket resolution, giving the business timely insight into service quality.

By configuring smart ticket routing (based on geography, form inputs, and category), we significantly reduced internal back-and-forth. The chatbot and connected Knowledge Base now serve as the first line of support for many users, streamlining human interaction to only the most relevant tickets.

While we’re still awaiting long-term statistics post go-live, early feedback indicates a noticeable drop in manual triaging and a faster time-to-resolution across support channels. The beam support pipeline went live in February, followed by installer support and ERP in staggered waves—allowing the Australian air conditioning and ventilation manufacturer’s internal team to adapt progressively.

The advisory sessions also uncovered opportunities for workflow cleanup and segmentation in other parts of the portal, providing additional value and a clear path forward.

### Impact

the Australian air conditioning and ventilation manufacturer’s transition to HubSpot was more than a platform switch—it was a strategic shift in how they deliver and manage customer support across the business. By consolidating their service operations into a single, fully operational HubSpot Service Hub instance, the Australian air conditioning and ventilation manufacturer gained the clarity, control, and collaboration needed to provide a seamless support experience.

Previously fragmented processes have now been unified into an automated, transparent system. Teams across support, sales, and operations are aligned through shared tools and real-time reporting dashboards, improving internal collaboration and accountability. With the new setup, Knowledge Base articles are intelligently tagged and chatbot-assisted, while ticketing is connected to workflows that guide users from issue to resolution with minimal friction.

Most notably, the Australian air conditioning and ventilation manufacturer’s internal teams now fully own the solution. With structured training and advisory support, internal champions can optimise workflows, onboard new team members, and adapt the system as business needs evolve—all without reliance on external resources.

From the customer’s perspective, the experience is intuitive and responsive. Whether it’s a homeowner with an Airtouch question or an installer looking for warranty assistance, queries are now routed quickly and accurately through simplified forms, aligned to SLA expectations, and followed by a clear feedback loop to continually improve service quality.

This new foundation gives the Australian air conditioning and ventilation manufacturer the ability to scale support operations confidently. As their products and teams grow, their service structure is ready to adapt, ensuring consistency, efficiency, and a support experience that evolves alongside their business.
