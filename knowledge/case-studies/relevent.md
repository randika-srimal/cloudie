### Challenge

Relevent set out to create a more connected, efficient, and compliant marketing engine to better serve their rapidly growing global fanbase. The team needed a platform that could support targeted communications at scale—tailored to the unique preferences and behaviours of each fan segment.

To meet their ambitious engagement goals, they sought a unified platform that could bring everything together—from campaign automation to audience insights.

A top priority was also creating a best-practice subscription management system—ensuring that fans could easily manage preferences while maintaining compliance with evolving global opt-in regulations. More than a technical upgrade, this initiative was about building the infrastructure to deliver seamless, fan-first experiences and setting the foundation for long-term digital growth.

### Solution

To address these challenges, we implemented HubSpot Marketing Hub to centralize and automate Relevent’s email marketing operations. Key initiatives included:

* **Data Migration:** Successfully imported and cleaned audience data from Dynamics, ensuring structured segmentation for personalised engagement. Additionally, after the migration, the Relevent team were able to send a few newsletters for IP warming to check their database health, allowing further cleanup in preparation for their Summer soccer matches.
* **Email Marketing Setup:** Developed and designed an email template aligned with Relevent’s brand guidelines, allowing for quick and scalable email deployment.
* **Audience Segmentation:** Built dynamic lists based on previous marketing contacts with scalability for future growth and segmenting by region and interest.
* **Lifecycle Stages & Subscription Types:** Established lifecycle stages to track audience engagement and subscription types for compliance and user preferences.
* **User Training & Enablement:** Conducted comprehensive training sessions for Relevent’s marketing team, enabling them to manage and optimize campaigns independently.

### Results

* **Improvement in audience engagement** due to automated workflows and lifecycle management.
* **Reduction in manual workload**, enabling the team to focus on strategic marketing initiatives.
* **Enhanced compliance with subscription management**, ensuring a streamlined opt-in process for fans.
* **Successful execution of initial email campaigns** for promotions, leading to increased event awareness and ticket sales.

### Impact

The implementation of HubSpot Marketing Hub marked a pivotal evolution in Relevent’s approach to fan engagement. With a centralised, intuitive platform now in place, the marketing team can deliver highly targeted, data-driven campaigns that resonate with distinct audience segments—from European soccer league supporters and beyond.

The introduction of robust subscription management and audience segmentation tools not only enhanced personalisation but also ensured compliance with evolving data regulations, delivering a smoother, more transparent user experience.

In the short term, the team saw faster campaign execution and increased engagement. Long term, the benefits are even more powerful: deeper audience insights, improved fan retention, and a scalable marketing automation framework designed to evolve alongside Relevent’s expanding international footprint.

This transformation has positioned Relevent to lead with precision, agility, and personalisation—ensuring their global events continue to connect with fans in more meaningful and measurable ways.
