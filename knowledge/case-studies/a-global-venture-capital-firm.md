### Challenge

With operations across more than 25 markets, the global venture capital firm had already established a strong global presence and a visionary approach to identifying and backing the world’s most driven founders. Their mission to support entrepreneurs from day zero was supported by a clear internal understanding of their customer journeys and touchpoints, and they had mapped out thoughtful processes to reflect this—particularly across Sales and Scouting teams.

The global venture capital firm had a wealth of valuable data and an ambitious strategy to nurture founder relationships at scale, and they were already leveraging platforms like Greenhouse and Airtable to support their workflows. However, the next step in their evolution was clear: unify these systems into a scalable CRM that could power both global consistency and local flexibility.

Their teams understood the importance of high-touch personalisation, region-specific workflows, and data-informed decision making. With detailed user personas, funnel diagrams, and nurturing strategies already in place, the global venture capital firm came to the project with a clear vision—they needed a platform that could consolidate their efforts, automate manual processes, and equip their globally distributed teams with the tools to act fast, personalise communication, and scale efficiently. Their focus was never just on technology—it was on building a foundation that could future-proof their ambitious growth plans.

### Solution

From day one, the global venture capital firm came to the table prepared. Armed with clear documentation in Figma and Miro, and a deep understanding of their internal workflows, they weren’t just ready for change—they were actively driving it. Their internal alignment and vision made this a true collaboration from the outset, allowing Engaging.io to hit the ground running.

The brief was ambitious: deploy a fully tailored HubSpot CRM that could scale across continents while supporting the global venture capital firm’s dual-track founder journey. We began by rolling out Sales Hub Professional and Marketing Hub Enterprise across three pilot regions—Australia, the US, and India.

To make it work, we engineered three distinct pipelines aligned to region and fund, with location-based routing rules and a custom object structure to replicate their Greenhouse “Jobs” data inside HubSpot. This allowed recruiters and scouts to track applicant progress with the precision of an ATS, but the flexibility of a CRM.

Behind the scenes, we built round-robin lead assignment workflows that ensured prospective founders were automatically routed to the right Scouting team member based on geography and deal stage. Lead scoring logic was also implemented—designed to spotlight the highest potential applicants based on their engagement, background, and founder archetype.

On the marketing front, we developed modular landing pages and email templates that could be reused and personalised across teams and territories. Subscription types and form logic were tailored to support regional preferences, campaign tracking, and GDPR compliance. The global venture capital firm's 12-month nurture journey was carefully mapped and automated, ensuring high-touch engagement with zero manual lift.

Data was another beast entirely. With over 300,000 records in Greenhouse, our team supported the global venture capital firm in a massive data hygiene and import process. Deals, contacts, custom objects, and historical applicant interactions were meticulously cleaned and imported—building a single source of truth from disparate spreadsheets and legacy systems.

And then there were the integrations.

To connect LaGrowthMachine, the global venture capital firm’s go-to for LinkedIn automation, we configured a HubSpot Marketplace integration and wrote custom field mappings to sync campaign data and contact replies. Contacts were only added to HubSpot once a reply was received in LinkedIn—ensuring Marketing Contact limits were respected and reducing CRM noise. We also built automation triggers to move those contacts into targeted nurture streams within HubSpot the moment they became engaged.

Haystack, the global venture capital firm’s LinkedIn scraping tool, presented a different kind of challenge. While not a direct integration, we helped the team establish a structured CSV import workflow, mapping scraped profile data into custom HubSpot properties. We also created visibility rules to ensure data stayed relevant to each region’s Scouting team, avoiding clutter or duplication.

Throughout the build, we empowered the global venture capital firm’s team with a train-the-trainer model, delivering deep-dive workshops, recorded sessions, and tailored cheat sheets. Local admins could now own their own regional rollout, with a scalable model ready to be cloned across 20+ additional markets.

It wasn’t just a CRM setup—it was a business enablement engine. Custom-built. Globally scalable. Fully the global venture capital firm.

### Results

For the global venture capital firm, the result wasn’t just a shiny new CRM—it was a structural shift in how they operate globally.

What once lived in sprawling spreadsheets, siloed platforms, and disconnected teams is now centralised within a single HubSpot ecosystem, giving the global venture capital firm an operational command centre across Australia, the US, and India—with the rest of the world ready to plug in next.

Visibility was the first big win. With over 300,000 founder records cleaned, structured, and imported from Greenhouse, the Scouting and Marketing teams now share one unified view of every founder—from the moment of first outreach, right through to offer acceptance and residency onboarding. No more double handling. No more “where did that lead go?” moments.

Time savings came next. With automated lead routing, round-robin assignment, and nurture journeys running in the background, team members are spending *less time chasing data* and *more time engaging with high-potential founders*. Manual handovers between Marketing and Scouting have been replaced with seamless workflow triggers, freeing up hours every week across each team.

Campaigns that used to take weeks—from audience segmentation to launch—are now templated, repeatable, and localised with a few clicks. Email engagement is up. Lead conversion velocity is improving. And for the first time, the global venture capital firm has real-time dashboards to track it all, broken down by region, fund, and founder archetype.

And perhaps the biggest shift? Confidence. The global venture capital firm’s team now has a platform that reflects the scale and ambition of their business. A system that can flex across 25 markets. A model that can be cloned, rolled out, and optimised by internal champions without waiting on external support.

In short: this wasn’t just a tech implementation. It was a foundational unlock. One that’s now powering faster decisions, smarter growth, and better founder experiences—right from day zero

### Impact

For the global venture capital firm, the ripple effects of this transformation were felt almost immediately—and they continue to grow stronger with every week.

Internally, the shift was palpable. What used to be fragmented, manual, and hard to scale has now become seamless, automated, and intelligent. The Scouting teams—who previously juggled multiple tools just to keep track of who was where in the pipeline—now work from a unified view that’s built around how they actually operate. They no longer waste time hunting down information or duplicating effort. With just a few clicks, they can see a founder’s full journey: what content they’ve engaged with, who spoke to them last, what their archetype is, and how close they are to signing.

The marketing team is now empowered. Thanks to scalable templates, strategic workflows, and a centralised database, they’ve moved from reactive, one-off campaigns to automated nurture streams that speak to founders at every stage of the funnel—across every region. And because performance is now measurable through tailored dashboards, the team can double down on what’s working and iterate on what’s not. That’s not just operational maturity—it’s strategic agility.

But the biggest winners? The founders themselves.

Gone are the disjointed follow-ups, missed connections, and generic outreach. Instead, founders are welcomed into a journey that feels considered, consistent, and—most importantly—human. The right conversations are happening at the right time. Communication is tailored, responsive, and backed by data. It’s the kind of experience that reflects the calibre of what the global venture capital firm promises: to be a true partner from day zero.

In the short term, Engaging.io helped the global venture capital firm regain hours, tighten processes, and scale outreach across three regions without missing a beat. In the long term, the foundation is now set for a truly global CRM operation. One that doesn't just keep up with the global venture capital firm’s growth—it enables it.

With a system that supports localisation, autonomy, and smart automation, the global venture capital firm’s team is no longer limited by their tools. They’re leading with them. And that’s exactly the kind of transformation we set out to deliver.
