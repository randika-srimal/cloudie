### Challenge

MARS Japan set out to enhance the speed, flexibility, and effectiveness of its marketing operations—starting with one of its most powerful engagement tools: coupons. As a key driver of customer acquisition and retention, coupon campaigns were central to their strategy. However, the existing Salesforce Marketing Cloud (SFMC) setup required developer intervention for each new campaign, limiting agility and slowing down execution.

With a vision to launch personalised, timely campaigns across a competitive retail landscape, the marketing team needed more autonomy to create and manage coupon promotions without relying on technical resources. The challenge wasn’t just about speed—it was about empowering the team to be more responsive to market trends and customer behaviours.

Compounding this was the fragmentation of data across systems, which made it difficult to track campaign performance or gather meaningful insights. MARS Japan aimed to implement a solution that would unify data, streamline coupon management, and ultimately, boost operational efficiency—laying the foundation for more dynamic and data-driven marketing.

### Solution

To address these challenges, we implemented a comprehensive digital transformation using HubSpot Marketing Hub Enterprise. Our approach included:

1. **Custom Store Portal Development:**
   * Built a user-friendly portal to serve as the central hub for coupon generation, distribution, and redemption tracking.
   * Provided store-specific and chain-wide coupon management capabilities.
   * Automated the coupon lifecycle, including expiration and validation, reducing reliance on developers.
2. **Seamless Integration:**
   * Integrated HubSpot with Salesforce and Spiral to ensure unified customer data.
   * Enabled real-time data synchronization, providing accurate insights into campaign performance.
3. **Automated Campaign Management:**
   * Implemented dynamic workflows to trigger personalized email campaigns based on customer behavior and lifecycle stages.
4. **Empowered Marketing Team:**
   * Conducted hands-on training sessions to ensure the marketing team could independently manage campaigns.

This tailored approach transformed HubSpot into a powerful coupon generation and redemption platform, allowing MARS Japan to execute campaigns with greater speed and agility.

### Results

The results of the project exceeded expectations. Key outcomes included:

* **Increase in Coupon Redemptions:** Enhanced visibility and automation led to significantly higher redemption rates.
* **Improved Operational Efficiency:** Automated data synchronisation reduced manual work, freeing up the marketing team to focus on strategic tasks.
* **Autonomous Marketing Operations:** The marketing team gained complete control over coupon management.
* **Data-Driven Decision-Making:** Centralised data enabled better campaign analysis and customer segmentation.

The integration with Salesforce also ensured seamless data flow, allowing MARS Japan to track campaign performance and coupon redemptions accurately.

### Impact

The transition to HubSpot marked a major shift for MARS Japan, empowering the marketing team to take full ownership of their coupon strategy. What once required developer intervention can now be executed independently, dramatically reducing campaign turnaround times and unlocking greater agility across the board.

Customers quickly experienced the benefits of this transformation. Tailored offers—such as growth-stage coupons for new pet owners—created more relevant, timely engagement and fostered deeper brand loyalty. With real-time redemption tracking in place, store managers were able to make data-informed decisions on the fly, optimising in-store strategies to align with customer behaviour and campaign performance.

Internally, the streamlined workflow has allowed marketing to focus more on creative execution and personalisation, rather than platform management. This newfound efficiency has laid the foundation for smarter segmentation, richer insights, and more targeted engagement.

Looking ahead, MARS Japan is now well-positioned to scale its campaigns with precision. With a unified data infrastructure and a flexible platform at their fingertips, the team is ready to deliver even more personalised, impactful experiences, driving growth while deepening customer connection.
