### Challenge

As a company committed to innovation and growth, Akolade identified the need to realign their CRM platform with their evolving business goals. Over several years of successful HubSpot use, natural shifts in their event strategy, customer profiles, and reporting needs had outpaced their original system setup.

Recognising the opportunity for improvement, Akolade proactively sought a modernised solution that would empower their marketing, sales, and production teams with cleaner data, smarter segmentation, and actionable insights. Rather than patch an ageing system, Akolade demonstrated strong leadership by opting to rebuild on a new HubSpot portal.

This strategic decision positioned them to elevate customer engagement, streamline internal workflows, and prepare for future growth with a more robust CRM foundation.

### Solution

Engaging.io worked closely with Akolade to design and deliver a full CRM revitalisation project, ensuring the new HubSpot portal not only matched but elevated their operational aspirations.

The project kicked off with in-depth discovery sessions to map out Akolade’s sales, marketing, production, and reporting processes. Our team configured HubSpot Marketing Hub Enterprise and Sales Hub Professional to align with Akolade’s segmented audience strategy and multi-domain requirements. We implemented:

* A fresh, structured deal pipeline across Sales, SPEX, Marketing, and Production teams.

* A tailored segmentation framework to accurately target diverse audiences, including delegates, sponsors, and speakers.

* Clean data migration protocols, ensuring only high-quality, actionable data was carried into the new portal.

* Smart workflows for lead assignment, lead nurturing, and quote approvals.

* Custom reporting dashboards providing real-time insights for Sales, Marketing, and Event Management.

Training sessions, both virtual and in-person, equipped Akolade’s teams to manage their new CRM with confidence. Our phased handover strategy ensured that Akolade became fully autonomous in their new platform environment.

### Results

Akolade now operates with a HubSpot portal that mirrors their operational excellence and event-driven agility. Immediate benefits included:

* **Significant data quality improvement**, with non-actionable contacts cleansed and streamlined for better segmentation.

* **Fully optimised pipelines** across key departments, enabling faster lead management and more accurate deal tracking.

* **Enhanced marketing performance** through refined segmentation strategies and smart automation, reducing manual campaign management efforts by an estimated 40%.

* **Dashboards built for insight**: Sales, Marketing, and Production teams can now track KPIs, deal conversion rates, event registration performance, and overall revenue in real time.

* **Empowered teams**: Through tailored training and process documentation, Akolade’s staff can manage, adapt, and scale their use of HubSpot without ongoing external dependency.

The completed system not only solved for today's needs but laid a strong foundation for Akolade’s evolving event and digital strategies.

### Impact

The new HubSpot environment has reshaped Akolade’s operational landscape in several ways.

In the **short term**, the immediate lift came from streamlined processes: deals moving faster through pipelines, marketing initiatives launched with greater speed and precision, and internal clarity around CRM usage. Staff across teams gained more visibility into key metrics, fostering a performance-driven culture grounded in data, not assumptions.

**Long term**, the biggest transformation lies in Akolade’s ability to scale with confidence. Their marketing and sales efforts are no longer constrained by legacy data issues or outdated workflows. The modular setup of pipelines, segmentation, and dashboards provides Akolade with the flexibility to easily expand into new event formats, geographic markets, or audience segments without additional rebuilds.

The ability to self-manage key aspects of HubSpot — from creating new workflows to adjusting deal stages — ensures that Akolade’s team remains nimble as business needs evolve. By investing in strong initial training and documentation, Akolade is set up for sustainable success, ensuring that the platform grows with them, not against them.

The engagement also sparked broader strategic conversations around audience engagement, marketing automation, and future systems integrations (e.g., deeper e-commerce connectivity and Xero integration), demonstrating Akolade’s commitment to continuous innovation.
