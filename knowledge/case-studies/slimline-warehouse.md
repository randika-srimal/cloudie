### Challenge (150 words)

Slimline Warehouse faced a dual-front challenge: inefficient sales processes and fragmented customer service systems.

* Their CRM lacked automation, leading to inconsistent follow-ups and bloated sales pipelines.
* Leads were assigned manually with no backup process during staff absences, often resulting in lost opportunities.
* The lack of funnel clarity made it hard to distinguish between active prospects and dead ends.

On the support side, customer tickets were scattered and manually triaged, with no visibility into status or urgency.

* There was no dedicated process for escalations, leading to delays in resolving complex issues.
*  Without a proper leave handover system, both sales and service queues were vulnerable to bottlenecks.

Slimline needed a connected, automated solution that could scale, reassign, and report — without the chaos.

### Solution

Engaging.io implemented HubSpot Sales Hub Professional and Service Hub Professional, integrating them with Slimline’s WMS for real-time data synchronization. Key implementations included:

1. **Sales Automation and Funnel Clarity:**
   * We re-architected Slimline’s lead management by automating the full sales journey — from lead capture through qualification, scoring, and assignment.
   * A dedicated track for pre-SQL leads helped clean out top-of-funnel noise, while SQLs were routed directly to reps, creating a focused and accountable pipeline.

2. **Enhanced Reporting & Analytics:**
   * Built a centralized dashboard for tracking deal flow, sales rep performance, and customer lifecycle stages.
   * Integrated custom sales goals for performance benchmarking.

3. **Customer Service Transformation:**
   * A dual-pipeline setup that splits general enquiries from escalations, allowing higher priority issues to bypass standard queues.
   * A helpdesk structure where tickets were assigned on a first-come, first-served basis to available agents.
   * Automation workflows for triaging and routing tickets based on urgency, category, and agent workload.
   * A dedicated leave-based reassignment system for customer service staff, mirroring the logic in sales.
     Escalations were held and tracked separately, giving managers clear visibility into high-risk issues.

4. **Website & Landing Pages:**
   * Migrated and redesigned 20 landing pages to improve conversion rates.

5. **Training & Onboarding:**
   * Conducted interactive training sessions to ensure adoption and best practices within the Slimline team.

### Results

* Increase in sales efficiency due to workflow automation.
* The separation of pre-SQL and SQL leads eliminated pipeline noise and improved targeting.
* The leave management logic ensured zero lead drop-off during staff absences, while ownership protocols preserved account continuity
* Real-time reporting, enabling better decision-making.
* A reduction in customer ticket resolution time improved customer satisfaction.
* Seamless WMS integration, reducing data discrepancies and manual work.
* Improved self-service support, reducing inbound service tickets by 20

### Impact

Slimline Warehouse's transformation has been both foundational and forward-looking. Their teams now operate in a digital ecosystem that’s automated, responsive, and perfectly aligned with their goals.

**For Sales**, the new workflows mean less admin, more selling. Reps log in and see clean, segmented pipelines, knowing that their queue reflects real-time, qualified leads. Automation ensures follow-ups are timely, and the system routes opportunities based on logic, not gut feel. The introduction of leave logic has been quietly powerful — keeping momentum alive even when staff rotate out.

Ownership logic has also had strategic value. Key accounts now remain with the right reps, even if reassignment occurs elsewhere. Child companies no longer fall through the cracks, as their relationships are tied to the right team members from the start.

**For Customer Support**, things are clearer and faster. Every ticket enters a structured journey — triaged, prioritised, and assigned without the daily scramble. First-come-best-dressed logic removed ambiguity in agent workflows, while escalations now live in their own pipeline, visible and trackable by management. This clarity alone has increased team confidence and reduced fire drills.

Add to this the leave management for agents, and you get a system that’s resilient, not reactive. Even during holidays or unexpected absences, customers remain supported — and it shows.

With HubSpot dashboards offering instant insights across every stage of sales and support, Slimline’s leadership now has the foresight and control needed to make informed, agile decisions.

In short: streamlined ops, empowered teams, and a setup built to scale.
