### Challenge

the retail advertising space business faced major operational bottlenecks due to reliance on MS Access and Zoho. Their sales and contract processes were highly manual, inefficient, and prone to errors. They struggled with:

* **Managing advertising inventory** (board slots) efficiently.
* **Tracking contract status** and ensuring smooth payment processing.
* **Lack of automation**, causing delays in deal closures.
* **Data duplication issues** due to multiple disconnected systems.
* **Limited marketing automation**, making lead nurturing ineffective.
* **No seamless integration for proof-of-placement tracking**, impacting customer transparency.

The sales team struggled with visibility into board inventory and contract management, leading to lost opportunities and slow deal processing. Marketing efforts lacked automation, making campaign execution inconsistent. Customer service was hampered by manual tracking of proof-of-placement and support tickets. Additionally, billing was handled outside the CRM, causing delays and reconciliation issues. The retail advertising space business needed an all-in-one system to unify operations, improve visibility, automate processes, and enable scalability.

### Solution

Our team implemented **HubSpot’s Marketing, Sales, Service, and Commerce Hubs** to centralise and automate the retail advertising space business’s operations. The solution included:

1. Custom Object Configuration:
   * Designed Board & Slot Custom Objects to track inventory availability, contracts, and payments.
   * Automated slot allocation & contract renewal reminders.
2. Commerce Hub & Payment Integration:
   * Implemented Commerce Hub for automated invoicing and payment processing.
   * Set up custom workflows to trigger payments based on proof-of-placement confirmation.
3. Marketing & Sales Hub Implementation:
   * Enabled automated lead nurturing campaigns via HubSpot workflows.
   * Created dynamic sales pipelines with automated follow-ups and notifications.
4. Service Hub & Proof of Placement Automation:
   * Integrated custom forms & ticketing system to track proof-of-placement.
   * Allowed real-time updates for clients on ad placements.
5. Data Migration & Training:
   * Migrated Zoho & MS Access data into HubSpot with de-duplication and clean-up.
   * Conducted training & documentation sessions for the retail advertising space business team.

### Results

the retail advertising space business experienced significant operational improvements post-implementation:

* Reduction in manual contract processing time due to automation.
* Increased sales efficiency, with real-time inventory visibility & automated workflows.
* Marketing engagement was boosted, leading to higher lead conversion rates.
* Customer experience improved, as proof-of-placement was now automated and transparent.
* Sales pipeline reporting enhanced, giving management better forecasting capabilities.

With our ongoing retainer, we continue to refine their workflows, provide training, and troubleshoot issues to ensure they maximise HubSpot’s capabilities.
