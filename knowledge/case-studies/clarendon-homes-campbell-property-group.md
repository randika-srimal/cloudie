## **Summary of the business:**

This case study focuses on phase one of the project, which involved setting up Clarendon Homes NSW in HubSpot.

### Challenge

Clarendon Homes NSW faced challenges with fragmented systems, manual processes, and inconsistent customer data, making it difficult to streamline operations, generate accurate reports, and maintain a seamless customer journey. Past CRM implementations fell short, leading to inefficiencies across sales, marketing, and operations.

Additionally, each business unit operated independently, creating complexities in standardising processes. A lack of internal expertise with HubSpot and the need for third-party integrations added another layer of difficulty. Clarendon Homes NSW required a comprehensive CRM solution to centralise customer data, improve reporting, and enhance overall business efficiencies.

### Solution

To address these challenges, a **phased approach** was implemented, starting with Clarendon Homes NSW as the pilot business unit. This ensured a controlled, scalable rollout across the broader organisation.

### Key Steps Taken:

* **CRM Implementation:** HubSpot Enterprise Suite was deployed, integrating Sales, Marketing, and Operations Hubs.
* **System Integrations:** Connections were established between Framework, ClickHome, SharePoint, and Smartsheet to streamline data management.
* **Data Migration and Cleansing:** Sales and customer data were carefully migrated to HubSpot, ensuring accuracy and usability.
* **Process Standardisation:** New workflows were developed to unify business processes, enhancing efficiency across departments.
* **Training & User Adoption:** Teams underwent structured onboarding and training sessions, ensuring the successful adoption of the new CRM.

By leveraging HubSpot’s automation and integration capabilitie**s**, the solution positioned Clarendon Homes NSW for long-term operational success while setting a framework for future scalability.

### Results

The initial rollout has shown positive early indicators, demonstrating tangible benefits:

* **Process Efficiency Gains:** Standardised CRM workflows have reduced administrative burden and manual data entry.
* **Data Visibility & Accuracy:** Improved data integrity and seamless integration with existing tech stacks.
* **User Adoption:** Teams have responded well to training, with increasing engagement in CRM tools.

As the project progresses, lead conversion rates, reporting accuracy and operational efficiency will be key metrics of success.

### Impact

The Phase 1 rollout has already transformed the way Clarendon Homes NSW operates by enhancing data-driven decision-making, streamlining workflows, and fostering better collaboration across departments. With improved CRM adoption, customer engagement and marketing personalisation have seen noticeable enhancements.

Automated workflows have also reduced administrative overhead, allowing the sales team to focus more on meaningful customer interactions. Additionally, better data consistency has improved forecasting and strategic planning, while enhanced tracking of customer interactions has led to more responsive and efficient service delivery.

The phased approach has enabled the business to refine processes in real-time, ensuring minimal disruption while maximising operational effectiveness.

Phase 1 has set a strong precedent for digital transformation at Clarendon Homes NSW. While long-term benefits such as cost savings and ROI are still being measured, the project has already delivered measurable improvements in business efficiency, customer experience, and operational effectiveness. As future phases roll out, the organisation is well-positioned to further enhance its competitive edge in the residential homebuilding industry.

### Future Growth

* Expanding the HubSpot rollout to other business units.
* Integrating advanced automation features for lead scoring and customer journey mapping.
* Exploring potential integrations with event management and financial services systems.
