### Challenge (150 words)

Cabcharge faced several challenges in streamlining its sales and account management processes. Their reliance on **Dynamics 365** led to siloed data, making it difficult for sales agents to track customer interactions effectively.

Additionally, the lack of an **intuitive CRM system** hindered their ability to **scale and manage** their growing customer base. Sales teams struggled with manual processes, impacting efficiency and customer engagement. Ensuring seamless integration between **HubSpot and Dynamics 365** was critical to maintaining operational consistency while improving overall sales performance.

### Solution (250 words)

Engaging.io implemented **HubSpot Sales Hub Professional**, and Operations Hub Starter, configuring it to align with Cabcharge’s existing sales workflows. A **single sales pipeline** was established to manage **8,500 active accounts**, ensuring proper visibility and tracking.

* **HubSpot’s native Dynamics 365 Connector** was utilized to enable **real-time data sync** without replacing Dynamics as the system of record.
* **Automation tools** within HubSpot streamlined lead assignment, deal tracking, and customer follow-ups.
* **Sales dashboards** were developed to provide insights into account performance, sales forecasts, and engagement metrics.
* **User training** sessions were conducted to ensure sales teams could effectively navigate and utilize HubSpot’s features.
  * Training also covered how to migrate complex data from D365 and Cabcharge’s bespoke payments system for account information into HubSpot.
  * Due to the custom set-up, training on data associations for parent and child accounts ensured confidence and user uptake in the system.
* The implementation of a **UAT Documentation Library** and a **Shared Inbox** improved collaboration and support processes.

### Results (250 words)

* **Increased CRM adoption** among sales teams, with **improved navigation** and ease of use.
* **50% reduction** in time spent on manual data entry due to automated lead assignments.
* **Real-time visibility** into sales activities through dashboards, enhancing decision-making.
* **Integration between HubSpot and Dynamics 365** ensured **data consistency** across both platforms.
* Enhanced **reporting capabilities**, providing insights into customer engagement and pipeline performance.
* Early signs of improved **conversion rates** due to better customer tracking and follow-ups.

### Impact (300 words)

The successful implementation of HubSpot Sales Hub Professional and Operations Hub transformed how Cabcharge manages its sales processes. Account managers now have centralised access to all customer interactions, improving efficiency and reducing errors.

Short-term improvements include:

* **Immediate time savings** for sales teams with automated workflows.
* **Better lead management** with clear pipeline tracking.
* **Increased engagement** with HubSpot Academy training ensuring ongoing platform use.

Long-term benefits:

* **Centralised data for accounts** ensuring accurate reports and providing confidence for users.
* Visual indicators and specialised automation for key accounts reduce the pressure for manual follow-ups and improve the customer experience.
* **Scalability**: The foundation is now set for **further HubSpot expansion**, including potential **Marketing Hub** integration.
* **Data-driven decision-making**: Improved **reporting capabilities** enable strategic planning and performance tracking.
* **Customer experience enhancements**: With better engagement tracking, sales teams can **nurture and retain key accounts more effectively.**
