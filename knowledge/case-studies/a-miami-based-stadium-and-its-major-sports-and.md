### Challenge

the stadium set out to unify and elevate its marketing operations across a dynamic, multi-brand environment. With a packed calendar of high-profile events and a diverse portfolio of brands operating under one roof, the team recognized the need for a more connected, scalable approach to customer engagement and data management.

Each brand needed autonomy for creative execution but demanded centralised oversight and infrastructure to manage shared fans and stakeholders. Timely segmentation, lifecycle management, and campaign personalisation were mission-critical for major events like the stadium's motorsport event and NFL season launches, where marketing speed directly impacted ticket sales and sponsorship opportunities.

Improved automation, lead scoring, lifecycle stages and website tracking were top of mind, along with the ability to make data-driven decisions faster through clearer reporting. With critical deadlines for major events on the horizon, the stadium’s internal teams were ready to invest in a platform that could streamline operations, support smarter segmentation that the marketing team could manage, and drive long-term digital transformation at the enterprise level.

### Solution

Engaging.io partnered with the stadium to design and execute a phased rollout strategy across four brands, leveraging HubSpot’s enterprise capabilities. Engaging.io built out the following Integrations that were critical to project success:

* **Databricks Integration**: Eliminating data silos and aligning ticketing, sponsorship, and marketing teams around a single source of truth.
* **Data Migration**: Migrated, mapped, and cleaned fragmented sponsorship, media, and event datasets. Consolidated into a unified CRM view to enable immediate, advanced segmentation and reporting across brands without disrupting live campaigns.
  * Getting data migration right was key to enabling the real-time, actionable insights the stadium wanted from the Databricks integration.

Other key elements of the solution included:

* **Phased Brand Launch**: The stadium's motorsport event launched first as a proof of concept, establishing data structures, integrations, and full campaign processes before scaling to the stadium's tennis tournament, the stadium's football team, and the stadium (Concerts).
* **Modular Systems**: Built brand-specific workspaces with dedicated branding, lead scoring, lifecycle stages, forms, subscription centers, and reporting dashboards.
* **Dynamic Content Strategy**: Developed modular email templates and dynamic content blocks, allowing each brand creative flexibility while maintaining consistency and operational efficiency.

This structure empowered the stadium to transition to a future-proof marketing engine that supports brand flexibility, operational efficiency, and data-driven decision making.

### Results

The results of this multi-brand rollout were transformative. Each brand is now live and running its own campaigns inside HubSpot, with common infrastructure powering divergent audience experiences. The Grand Prix launched first, setting the standard with lead scoring, lifecycle management, nurture workflows, and integrated data flows from numerous 3rd party platforms.

Contact data is now unified and enriched—each brand can segment and engage their audiences in real time, while leadership can access roll-up reporting across brands. Teams report 40–50% reductions in campaign setup time thanks to reusable assets and automation.

* Real-time segmentation became a reality with Databricks integration, allowing timely, targeted outreach for major events.
* Unified CRM visibility across ticketing, sponsorships, and marketing operations boosted cross-team collaboration.

The shift from siloed operations to a shared digital backbone has dramatically increased agility. Campaigns are more targeted, data is actionable, and fan engagement is stronger and more relevant.

By investing in a platform-first strategy, the stadium has not only improved operations across individual brands but also positioned itself as a leader in digital fan engagement across the global sports and entertainment landscape.

### Impact

The implementation of HubSpot, bolstered by powerful integrations and thoughtful data migration, ushered in a new era of operational excellence for the stadium.

Short-term, marketing and sponsorship teams now execute campaigns faster, segment audiences more accurately, and collaborate seamlessly across departments. Fan engagement is more relevant and data-backed, enhancing customer loyalty and event profitability.

Long-term, the integrated CRM foundation provides the stadium with a scalable infrastructure to launch new brands, events, and loyalty initiatives without rebuilding core systems. Predictive modelling through Databricks fuels smarter marketing decisions, while real-time sales insights via Dynamics drive better sponsorship and VIP sales strategies.

Crucially, the stadium’s internal teams are now empowered to operate proactively rather than reactively, positioning the venue as a global leader in digital fan engagement and event marketing excellence.

As the flagship NFL franchise under the stadium umbrella, the stadium's football team needed a system that could power complex marketing and sales journeys across multiple fan segments—from single-game buyers to season ticket members and premium hospitality clients. January 2025 – March 2025

Sports & Entertainment (NFL)

**Licensing/ Key Products:**

* HubSpot Marketing Hub Enterprise
* Ops Hub Professional

Dynamics Connector

**Results:**

The stadium's football team’ marketing team gained full visibility across contact lifecycle stages, launched tailored campaigns for STM renewals and upsells, and aligned lead flows across marketing and sales teams via automated triggers and lifecycle scoring.

**Award Category:** Platform Excellence

### Challenge

the stadium's football team were ready to elevate their fan engagement strategy with a more connected, data-driven approach. With contacts flowing in from multiple channels—web forms, digital ads, newsletters, and more—the team saw an opportunity to unify their systems and create a streamlined path from first interaction to loyal season ticket holder.

Their goal was to better understand and segment their diverse fan base, from casual supporters to high-value prospects, and introduce automation to support timely, personalised communication at scale. They envisioned a smarter lifecycle tracking system, one that could surface sales-ready leads, trigger nurture sequences, and help marketing and sales work in sync.

They also recognised the value of campaign scalability. Rather than relying on one-off promotions, the team sought to build a sustainable engine for cross-sell, upsell, and renewal initiatives—all backed by real-time insights. With leadership looking to optimise performance through data, the football team needed a platform that could provide visibility into funnel metrics, ROI, and channel attribution, fueling both strategic planning and game-day readiness.

### Solution

We approached Phase 2 by implementing a tailored HubSpot Marketing Hub Enterprise instance for the football team business unit, with a focus on integrating data flow between HubSpot, Dynamics, and their external data lake in Databricks. A full lifecycle stage model was mapped specifically to the football team fan journey, distinguishing between newsletter subscribers, leads, MQLS, opportunities, and customers.

Lead scoring was designed around key engagement actions such as ticket purchase history, form fills, email interaction, and proximity to the stadium, ensuring that high-intent contacts would automatically be surfaced to sales via HubSpot workflows.

To manage complex renewal and upsell processes, we implemented nurture workflows that activated based on contact behaviour, membership status, and event proximity. Email templates were built around key moments—renewal reminders, upgrade opportunities, parking notifications, and exclusive STM perks—with dynamic personalisation enabled via HubSpot properties.

These journeys helped reduce manual campaign build time and ensured timely, relevant communications to each segment.

Custom dashboards and campaign reporting provided marketing and sales with real-time insight into lead movement, engagement, and campaign ROI. HubSpot’s integration with Dynamics ensured that sales reps had access to up-to-date lead data, while suppression lists and opt-in management helped reduce email fatigue and respect user preferences. Altogether, these solutions delivered a scalable, automated foundation for engaging fans year-round, not just during game season.

### Results

With HubSpot fully deployed, the football team marketing team launched a series of highly targeted campaigns that aligned perfectly with their STM cycle. Renewal emails were automatically sent based on membership status, while upgrade offers were triggered based on ticket scan data, purchase history, and engagement signals. Leads moved more predictably through the funnel, with dynamic scoring helping prioritise outreach. Email open and click-through rates improved due to better segmentation and personalisation, and the volume of manual list building dropped significantly.

Marketing gained the tools to build complex journeys once and reuse them for different cohorts. Sales benefited from clearer visibility into which leads were “warm” and ready for outreach. Real-time integration with Dynamics ensured data stayed in sync and allowed reps to act on leads faster. Workflows helped ensure contacts weren't lost between systems or teams, and time-to-first-touch decreased noticeably during peak campaigns.

Reporting dashboards provided a single source of truth for leadership, enabling weekly reviews of lead performance, campaign attribution, and conversion rates. Previously, ad hoc insights could now be consistently reviewed, driving better spend allocation and creative optimisation. All of this positioned the football team for a stronger sales cycle going into the next NFL season, with automated systems reducing workload and boosting responsiveness.

### Impact

The HubSpot implementation marked a new era of fan engagement for the stadium's football team—transforming their marketing from campaign-based outreach to a full-funnel, lifecycle-led strategy. With automation, segmentation, and real-time insights now embedded in their workflow, the team can guide fans through personalised journeys aligned with key milestones and behaviours throughout the season.

One of the most valuable outcomes was the strengthened alignment between marketing and sales. Shared lifecycle definitions, lead scoring, and performance dashboards enabled both teams to operate with clarity and consistency. Sales reps gained valuable context for outreach, while marketing could quickly adapt campaigns based on feedback and results, fostering a more collaborative, agile environment.

Operationally, the shift was just as impactful. Modular templates and automated workflows accelerated campaign execution, which proved especially powerful during high-priority moments like renewals and game-day activations. HubSpot’s advanced segmentation capabilities also allowed the team to engage VIPs, re-engage lapsed fans, and convert new prospects with precision and relevance.

From the fan’s perspective, the experience became more timely, tailored, and thoughtful. Communications felt intentional—aligned with individual interests and actions rather than broad pushes. Whether receiving an upsell offer or a support follow-up, each interaction felt part of a connected journey.

With a future-ready platform in place, the football team are now positioned to scale personalisation, test new acquisition strategies, and connect fan experiences across channels and seasons. The foundation is set not just for marketing efficiency, but for long-term digital leadership in the world of sport and entertainment.

### Challenge

With the stadium's tennis tournament fast approaching, the marketing team was focused on creating a unified, scalable engine to power their campaigns and deepen audience engagement. They needed to move quickly—establishing a centralised system to manage contacts, segment by interest (from general admission to hospitality and premium add-ons), and route leads effectively to sales and support teams.

Their vision was clear: deliver a seamless experience for fans while streamlining campaign execution behind the scenes. To achieve this, the team aimed to improve visibility into customer journeys, introduce lifecycle tracking, and implement smarter lead management. Managing global preferences and consent in a compliant, user-friendly way was also a key priority.

The challenge wasn’t just technical—it was about building the infrastructure to support real-time responsiveness, cross-team coordination, and personalised communication, all under the pressure of a live event cycle. The stadium's tennis tournament team needed a flexible, fast-to-deploy platform that could adapt to their pace and scale with confidence.

### Solution

Phase 3 focused on rapidly configuring HubSpot to support the fast-paced, high-touch needs of the stadium's tennis tournament. The project began with configuring a dedicated BU and setting up custom lifecycle stages for different tennis audience types—from newsletter subscribers to luxury suite buyers. We introduced lead scoring to help identify high-intent contacts engaging with premium ticket content and ensured they were surfaced to the sales team promptly.

Dynamic email templates were created and grouped into journeys tied to key moments in the fan journey: pre-sale registration, ticket release announcements, last-call promos, and “know before you go” event guides. With segmentation powered by custom properties like past attendance and interest type, the team could trigger highly personalised outreach.

HubSpot forms were embedded across owned properties and social lead ads, with all submissions funnelling into one contact record. Opt-in consent and preference centre management were also implemented to streamline communication preferences across global audiences.

The system was integrated with Dynamics to ensure sales reps had full visibility and could follow up quickly. Dashboards were built to track form fills, campaign engagement, and overall fan journey progression, enabling the marketing team to focus on optimisation instead of manual work.

### Results

the stadium's tennis tournament went live in time for their March tournament, with automated nurture journeys and lead capture forms already delivering warm leads to sales. Campaigns that had previously taken weeks to coordinate were now launched in days, thanks to reusable templates and automated workflows. The marketing team gained the ability to segment fans based on ticket interest and engagement level, sending targeted communications that aligned with real-time purchase behaviour.

The introduction of lifecycle tracking meant that every contact’s stage—from subscriber to MQL to purchaser—was visible, reducing confusion around campaign targeting. Lead scoring surfaced high-value prospects to sales reps in near real-time, improving response times and increasing conversion potential during high-demand periods.

Campaign performance dashboards provided leadership with visibility into how each initiative contributed to sales and engagement. Reports that had once taken hours to compile were now automated and available on demand, helping stakeholders prioritise spend and effort effectively.

The move away from disconnected tools to HubSpot enabled the stadium's tennis tournament to communicate with precision and agility. Contact records were centralised, campaigns became repeatable, and both the marketing and sales teams had access to the same live data, transforming the way they operated during one of the busiest periods of the year.

### Impact

the stadium's tennis tournament’s HubSpot implementation landed at exactly the right moment—just in time to support their highest-stakes ticketing window of the year. With the new platform in place, the marketing team shifted from reactive campaign execution to a proactive, data-driven strategy. Automation lifted the operational load, enabling the team to focus more on content, creativity, and performance optimisation.

One of the most immediate and impactful outcomes was a boost in operational efficiency. Previously, campaign execution involved coordinating lists across multiple platforms, manual UTM tagging, and time-consuming internal handovers. Post-implementation, modular templates, automated triggers based on lifecycle stage and interest, and streamlined processes drastically reduced production time. This freed up space for innovation, including cross-sell campaigns and influencer-led engagement.

Sales teams also gained a clear advantage. With prioritised leads surfaced through lifecycle tracking and lead scoring, they could focus on high-value conversations, particularly important for time-sensitive premium seating offers. Real-time data sync between HubSpot and Dynamics ensured alignment across marketing and sales, making collaboration smoother and more informed.

From the fan’s perspective, the shift was just as impactful. Communications became more timely, relevant, and personalised, enhancing the overall event experience whether they were first-time attendees or long-time VIPS. The result was stronger engagement and improved conversion during the most critical part of the sales cycle.

Beyond the tournament, the project has laid a strong, scalable foundation for future campaigns, off-season engagement, and deeper audience segmentation, setting the stadium's tennis tournament team up for long-term success.
