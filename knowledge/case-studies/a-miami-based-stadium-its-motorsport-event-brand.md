### Challenge

As the stadium's motorsport event prepared for another high-octane season, the marketing team set their sights on building a more connected, data-driven approach to fan engagement. With multiple platforms in play—including Dynamics, ZoomInfo, Meta, and LinkedIn—they wanted a centralised system that could streamline campaign execution, track pre-sale momentum, and surface actionable insights in real time.

Their goal was to create tailored journeys for different audiences—subscribers, leads, and ticket buyers—while equipping the sales team with relevant, high-quality leads without overwhelming capacity. The seasonal nature of the event added urgency to the project: systems needed to be live and functional well ahead of ticket launches to support pre-sale interest, hospitality upsells, and timely follow-up.

Improved coordination between marketing, sales, and external data partners like Ticketmaster was also a priority. The team sought greater control over lead capture, consent management, and attribution tracking—laying the groundwork for a more personalised, efficient fan experience and better visibility into campaign performance.

### Solution

We kicked off Phase 1 by aligning the motorsport event’s tech stack with HubSpot, connecting their data via Databricks and layering on a native Dynamics integration for sales. With HubSpot Marketing Hub Enterprise, we transitioned 10+ lead capture forms into the platform, capturing submissions from websites, Meta, and LinkedIn into one consistent funnel.

We configured lifecycle stages to reflect business unit journeys, built custom dashboards, and introduced lead scoring models tailored for subscribers and leads. This helped the team understand when a subscriber became sales-ready, triggering automated handoffs.

We also built out nurture journeys for pre-sale interest, hospitality offers, and final countdown campaigns leading up to race day. Every engagement – from email clicks to chatbot conversations – fed into lead scoring. Integration with Ticketmaster was scoped for future automation, but we began by manually syncing critical data for upsell journeys.

Email templates and branding assets were standardised, and segmentation based on interest (hospitality, grandstand, parking) was enabled using custom properties. Marketing and sales could now collaborate using shared dashboards and lifecycle models, giving them a 360-degree view of prospects.

### Results

By the time the first pre-sale campaign launched in November, the motorsport event BU was running a full-funnel system in HubSpot. Forms were embedded across platforms and captured in real-time, populating segmented lists by interest type. The initial nurture flow saw strong engagement, with high open and click-through rates, and hundreds of contacts progressing from subscriber to lead within weeks.

Hospitality interest was flagged through form fills and scoring triggers, with warm leads routed to sales in under five minutes thanks to the Dynamics integration. In one campaign alone, the sales team successfully identified and followed up with high-intent leads. Automated lifecycle flows also reduced manual effort, allowing the team to shift focus to strategy over execution.

Custom dashboards helped track campaign performance and form conversions, making it easier for stakeholders to prioritise spend and audiences. Previously untracked channels like Meta and LinkedIn were now clearly tied to contact acquisition and conversion. Cross-channel consistency improved brand presence and increased engagement during the high-demand sales window.

### Impact

In under 10 weeks, the stadium's motorsport event team transformed their marketing operations—moving from a fragmented tech stack to a streamlined, high-performing ecosystem within HubSpot. This shift didn’t just improve how leads were captured; it redefined how they were nurtured, segmented, and converted.

Pre-sale interest forms were seamlessly integrated into automated workflows, eliminating the need for manual handovers and ensuring timely, relevant follow-up from the moment a fan submitted their details. With behavioural segmentation and lifecycle tracking in place, marketing content became more personalised and strategic—driving stronger engagement and deeper fan connections.

A custom lead scoring model ensured only the most sales-ready contacts were passed on, helping the sales team prioritise their outreach and respond with speed and precision. Campaign performance insights, once difficult to access, became readily available through omnichannel dashboards—giving leadership clear visibility into ROI and informing next-season planning with confidence.

The internal impact was equally impressive. Shared access to a centralised toolset fostered tighter collaboration between teams, while automation and reusable templates cut campaign build time by more than 40%. The entire marketing cycle became more agile, efficient, and aligned.

From the fan’s perspective, the experience was smooth and engaging—communications were timely, tailored, and connected from the first click to the final purchase. This not only boosted conversion metrics but also helped position the stadium's motorsport event as a leader in digital fan engagement within highly competitive live event landscape.
