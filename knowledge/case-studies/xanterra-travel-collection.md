### Challenge

Xanterra approached this project with a bold vision: to transform a fragmented marketing ecosystem into a unified, data-driven powerhouse. With seven distinct brands—from iconic national park lodges to scenic railways—they recognised the untapped potential of aligning their marketing operations across a single platform.

Legacy systems like RedPoint had served them well for years, but they knew that to deliver the personalisation, segmentation, and scalability their audiences deserved, they needed to evolve. Each business unit had its own tech stack, data flows, and campaign structures, which made it difficult to gain a holistic view of the customer journey or execute coordinated cross-brand initiatives.

Rather than settle for status quo, Xanterra took a strategic leap, prioritising integration, automation, and enterprise-wide collaboration. It was an ambitious challenge—one requiring both technical precision and a deep commitment to change—but one they were fully prepared to tackle head-on.

### Solution

We implemented a comprehensive HubSpot onboarding across all seven business units, carefully designed to support Xanterra’s decentralised brand structure while enabling cross-brand visibility and scalable automation. The first stage was discovery—deep dives with each brand’s marketing leads and stakeholders to understand segmentation needs, email strategies, reporting requirements, and data sources.

We set up the HubSpot Marketing Hub Enterprise account with:

* Seven fully branded business units
* Custom subscription centres and email domains
* Progressive form strategy to improve opt-ins and data quality
* Replacement of Kentico and WordPress forms with HubSpot forms
* Marketing automation including nurture flows based on lifecycle and behaviour
* Custom templates and modular email designs for rapid campaign creation
* Integration support with Segment (led by McGaw), creating mapped identity flows and enabling events to sync between platforms
* Training sessions and flow mapping to empower internal teams to manage campaigns, contacts, and reporting independently

The onboarding followed our proven methodology with clear checkpoints: kickoff, configuration, training, go-live, and hypercare. With the first BU (Yellowstone) set to go live in early 2025, all teams have received foundational enablement.

### Results

Xanterra now has a scalable marketing platform to manage all of their BUs from a single pane of glass. Key achievements include:

* 7 business units implemented in HubSpot with custom branding and domain configurations
* Over 25 nurture workflows designed, prioritising enterprise-level journey management
* Custom objects scoped to handle bookings, packages, and upsell triggers from Segment and RMS data
* Setup for bi-directional sync with Segment to ensure data fidelity
* Enhanced email deliverability via IP warming strategy across all BUs
* Implemented scalable permissioning and team training model to support long-term independence
* Improved transparency into customer journey and campaign performance through consolidated reporting dashboards

Early indicators show that internal confidence in the platform is strong, and the team is already moving towards operationalising cross-sell campaigns.

### Impact

In the short term, Xanterra’s marketing teams can now run faster, smarter, and with more control. They’ve moved from disparate systems and unstructured processes to a single, integrated HubSpot ecosystem where they can track, segment, and respond to user behaviour across brands and properties. This has directly improved campaign turnaround times, reduced reliance on developers, and empowered marketers to act on insights in real-time.

Over the long term, this foundation will enable Xanterra to:

* Build lifecycle-based lead nurturing tailored to channel, loyalty tier, or trip history
* Create enterprise-level campaigns that simultaneously serve brand-specific and cross-sell goals
* Move toward centralised reporting that ties email engagement, web traffic, and booking data into a single dashboard

The project also laid the groundwork for a future sales integration and ticketing model, with scoped flexibility to accommodate evolving needs like AI-enabled chat and Cresta chat sync.
