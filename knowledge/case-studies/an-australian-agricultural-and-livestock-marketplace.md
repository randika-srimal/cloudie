### Challenge

As the Australian agricultural and livestock marketplace continued to grow as Australia’s leading digital marketplace for livestock and agricultural sales, the team identified several areas where their existing systems were limiting scalability and customer engagement. With an ambition to deliver even more personalised, data-driven experiences, they recognised the need for stronger CRM data integrity, more precise audience segmentation, and more efficient internal workflows.

Integrations with existing tools—such as their ticketing system and communications platforms—needed to be streamlined to reduce manual processes and unlock automation potential. Their deal management setup also required greater clarity and structure to better support both retention strategies and new business growth. The challenge wasn’t about fixing what was broken—it was about evolving systems to match the pace of their innovation and ambition.

### Solution

To address these challenges, Engaging.io conducted a comprehensive HubSpot audit and proposed a multi-phase implementation strategy:

1. **Data Cleanup & Deduplication:** Used a structured approach to consolidate customer records, set primary identifiers, and automate data hygiene processes.
2. **CRM Integration & Optimisation:** Integrated the Australian agricultural and livestock marketplace’ data warehouse with HubSpot, enabling real-time data sync for improved customer insights and reporting.
3. **Pipeline & Workflow Enhancements:** Redesigned sales and operations pipelines, creating dedicated retention and new business funnels. Introduced a lead qualification process before deal creation to improve accuracy.

### Results

* **Approximately 5% Reduction** in duplicate contacts, improving CRM accuracy.
* **Increase** in marketing campaign engagement due to refined audience segmentation.
* **Streamlined sales processes**, allowing better tracking of retention and new business opportunities.
* **Improved integration** between HubSpot and internal systems, reducing manual data entry.
* **Optimised telephony & SMS solutions**, leading to faster response times for customer inquiries.

### Impact

The project significantly improved the Australian agricultural and livestock marketplace' data quality, allowing them to leverage HubSpot’s automation and reporting features more effectively. Sales teams can now strategically engage with leads, thanks to clearer pipelines and automated workflows. Marketing teams benefit from better audience segmentation, driving higher conversion rates. Customer support has also been enhanced, with a more streamlined ticketing system and improved communication tools.

In the long term, these changes will enable the Australian agricultural and livestock marketplace to scale efficiently, leveraging data-driven decision-making to expand its market presence. The improved CRM structure sets a foundation for future enhancements, including deeper analytics and AI-driven personalisation.

This project transformed the Australian agricultural and livestock marketplace’ HubSpot environment into a well-integrated, efficient, and scalable system, empowering their teams to drive better customer engagement and business growth.
