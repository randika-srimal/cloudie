### Challenge

LPW faced several operational inefficiencies due to disconnected systems and manual workflows. The firm relied on Advisor Logic and Mailchimp for CRM and marketing but lacked automation and structured segmentation.

Lead qualification was inconsistent, with no clear handoff between marketing and sales. Additionally, LPW’s advisors needed better tracking of client engagement and more efficient client communication tools. Their reliance on spreadsheets and manual tracking limited their ability to scale and personalise their client interactions.

### Solution

Engaging.io implemented HubSpot Sales, Marketing, and Service Hub Professional to centralise LPW’s customer data, automate workflows, and improve lead management. A key integration was **JotForm**, which enabled seamless pre-appointment fact-find forms that auto-populated HubSpot, ensuring advisors had all relevant client information before meetings.

To improve efficiency, we built **automated lead segmentation** using HubSpot lists to ensure tailored communication. Marketing workflows were optimised to nurture leads over extended periods, accommodating LPW’s long sales cycles.

For client servicing, we structured a **ticketing system** to track ongoing client interactions and client reviews. We also trained LPW’s team in HubSpot best practices, ensuring long-term adoption and maximised ROI.

### Results

The HubSpot implementation significantly streamlined LPW’s operations. **Key results include:**

* Reduction in manual data entry with automated JotForm-HubSpot integration
* Faster lead qualification process with automated segmentation
* Increased client engagement through personalised email workflows
* Centralised pipelines, improving advisor visibility on client interactions

The successful implementation of HubSpot has given LPW a scalable, automated, and insight-driven platform that aligns with its business growth objectives. Their improved efficiency and enhanced customer engagement set them up for long-term success in the financial advisory industry.

### Impact

The transformation of LPW’s operations has enhanced both its internal efficiency and client experience. Advisors now have a clear view of client interactions, allowing for more personalised financial guidance.

Marketing campaigns are now data-driven, enabling the team to create highly targeted content based on segmented audiences. Advisors can now track which leads are engaging with content, allowing for proactive client follow-ups. The new centralised deal and ticket pipelines have improved business decision-making, providing insights into client trends and financial interests.

In phase two of the project, an integration with Advisor Logic will provide a single source of truth and eliminate duplication of actions and data. This ensures advisors and support teams are aligned, which will ultimately provide a better experience for clients. The automation of post-meeting tasks and reminders has also improved client retention and follow-up processes.
