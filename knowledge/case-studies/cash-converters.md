### Challenge

Cash Converters has always been a forward-thinking brand, continuously looking for ways to evolve their customer engagement strategies. As their business grew and customer expectations shifted, they recognised an opportunity to modernise their marketing operations. Their trusted Adobe Campaign platform had served them well, but with its end-of-life approaching, Cash Converters proactively sought a future-ready solution that could better align with their Single Customer View (SCV) strategy and support a new era of personalised marketing.

They were focused on enhancing segmentation capabilities, streamlining campaign management, and expanding into omnichannel communications—including SMS—without adding complexity for their teams. The goal was clear: empower their marketing team with tools that offered greater agility, scalability, and deeper insights, all while continuing to deliver outstanding customer experiences across their 160+ store network.

### Solution

Engaging.io delivered a comprehensive onboarding and migration project, grounded in our proven HubSpot implementation methodology.

The project began with deep discovery workshops across Cash Converters' Marketing and IT teams, mapping business processes, SCV structures, and campaign workflows. We then configured HubSpot Marketing Hub Enterprise and Operations Hub Professional, setting up advanced nurture workflows, segmentation based on SCV data, and dynamic content rules. Key highlights included:

* Migrating marketing processes into HubSpot with SCV-driven data architecture.

* Creating modular email and landing page templates for easy scaling.

* Setting up integrations with MessageMedia for transactional and marketing SMS.

* Building out smart reporting dashboards, campaign attribution models, and contact lifecycle tracking.

* Conducting detailed "train-the-trainer" sessions to empower internal teams to confidently manage and evolve their HubSpot environment.

Throughout the process, we focused on knowledge transfer, ensuring that Cash Converters' marketing team became fully self-sufficient with HubSpot by project end. A short Hypercare period followed to fine-tune systems and resolve post-Go-Live questions.

### Results

By project completion, Cash Converters had achieved a fully centralised marketing operation within HubSpot, eliminating the complexity and inefficiency of their old stack. Notable outcomes included:

* Significant reduction in time-to-launch for new marketing campaigns.

* 100% migration of existing templates, workflows, and segmentation structures.

* Full visibility of customer engagement through custom-built SCV dashboards.

* Seamless SMS integration enables timely transactional and marketing communications.

* Empowered marketing teams to manage campaigns independently without IT reliance.

The success of the Hallmark Campaign—Cash Converters' first major initiative post-migration—demonstrated the real-time agility gained through HubSpot, with enhanced reporting enabling immediate optimisation based on live results.

The project also significantly boosted internal collaboration between Marketing and IT, with improved data flows and fewer manual interventions.

### Impact

In the short term, Cash Converters experienced immediate marketing improvements: faster campaign rollouts, enhanced customer targeting, and actionable insights from their data. The shift to HubSpot meant the marketing team could independently create, launch, and measure campaigns without technical bottlenecks, freeing up IT resources for strategic initiatives.

The SCV integration meant that for the first time, Cash Converters had a truly unified view of customer journeys across stores and digital touchpoints. By connecting behavioural data with marketing activities, they unlocked new segmentation strategies, enabling highly relevant and personalised communications that are proven to drive customer loyalty.

Long-term, the modular structure of the templates and workflows set Cash Converters up for scalable growth. Whether adding new stores, launching new products, or expanding into new services, their HubSpot platform is flexible enough to adapt quickly.

The implementation also strengthened compliance and data governance practices, a critical aspect given the legislative oversight in financial services.

Thanks to the successful training and "train-the-trainer" model, Cash Converters' internal teams are not only maintaining the system but actively optimising it, with a strong feedback loop into new initiatives like abandoned cart recovery, upsell workflows, and loyalty program enhancements.

Ultimately, the HubSpot transformation has equipped Cash Converters with a future-proof platform, empowering their marketing to move as fast as their customers—and the market—demand.
