### Challenge

SUMO set out to modernise and unify its marketing operations to better support business growth and customer engagement. Their existing setup—built around Salesforce Marketing Cloud, Brave, and Single View—had served its purpose but required significant manual effort to execute campaigns and access performance data. The team recognised an opportunity to move toward a more streamlined, automated approach that could support scale without increasing operational overhead.

Their vision was to improve campaign agility, gain real-time insights, and establish a more connected experience between marketing and sales. With incomplete integrations across platforms, data was siloed and customer journeys were often disjointed—making it difficult to deliver consistent messaging or measure impact effectively.

To unlock their next stage of growth, SUMO needed a centralised solution that would not only simplify campaign management but also provide a seamless flow of data across systems. Automation, integration, and visibility were key—laying the foundation for more efficient collaboration and more personalised customer experiences.

### Solution (250 words)

Engaging.io implemented HubSpot Marketing Hub to address SUMO’s challenges, focusing on automation and system integration. The project began with discovery workshops to understand the marketing team’s needs and existing technology stack. A comprehensive solution was developed, encompassing HubSpot setup, workflow automation, and data integration.

Key deliverables included:

* **Nurture Workflows**: Optimised 10 workflows for lead nurturing and segmentation.
* **Integration with Azure SQL Database**: Using Census, we synchronised customer data between SUMO’s internal systems and HubSpot in real-time.
* **Custom APIS and Webhooks**: Enabled seamless data transfer for opt-in and opt-out preferences from HubSpot to Azure.
* **Marketing Attribution Dashboards**: Provided real-time visibility into campaign performance.
* **Team Training**: Delivered hands-on training sessions, empowering SUMO's team to manage campaigns independently and efficiently.

Our strategy ensured a smooth transition from Salesforce to HubSpot while equipping the team with the tools and knowledge needed for long-term success.

### Results (250 words)

The HubSpot implementation delivered immediate benefits, significantly enhancing SUMO’s marketing operations. Manual tasks were replaced with automated workflows, reducing human error and freeing the marketing team to focus on strategy and creativity. The integration with Azure SQL Database facilitated real-time data synchronisation, ensuring consistency across systems and improving data reliability.

Key results include:

* **Time Savings**: Automated processes reduced manual effort.
* **Improved Data Visibility**: Custom reporting dashboards provided management with actionable insights, enabling data-driven decision-making.
* **Enhanced Campaign Performance**: With better segmentation and nurture workflows, campaign engagement rates have increased.
* **Team Empowerment**: Hands-on training sessions built internal expertise, enabling the SUMO team to confidently manage HubSpot and reduce dependency on external support.

These improvements led to more cohesive marketing operations and a better customer experience across all touchpoints.

### Impact

The implementation of HubSpot brought a transformative shift to SUMO’s marketing operations—streamlining processes, improving data visibility, and empowering the team to focus on high-impact initiatives. With automation now driving much of the day-to-day execution, the marketing team reclaimed valuable time to focus on strategy, creative development, and innovation.

Real-time data integration through Azure ensured that customer information remained current and synchronised across systems, enabling more accurate targeting and eliminating communication gaps. Each touchpoint became more informed, consistent, and personalised—enhancing the overall customer experience. Newly implemented nurture workflows also unlocked smarter segmentation, allowing SUMO to deliver timely, relevant messaging that resonates across diverse audience segments.

In the immediate term, the team saw a measurable improvement in campaign efficiency, responsiveness, and engagement. But the long-term benefits are even more significant. With a scalable, flexible platform now in place, SUMO is positioned to grow its marketing program confidently—launching B2B campaigns, integrating new lead sources like TikTok, and expanding segmentation strategies as the business evolves.

Equipped with comprehensive training from Engaging.io, SUMO’s internal team is now self-sufficient and confident using HubSpot. They can manage campaigns independently, optimise performance in real time, and adapt the platform as new opportunities arise—ensuring the solution continues to drive value well into the future.
